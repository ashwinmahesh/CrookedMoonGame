Drafted by Gemini through ai-bridge (model requested: gemini-4-argon-medium; the bridge reports no model name back). The `crooked-moon` project refused the send (HTTP 400), so the brief went to project `ai-bridge` as lane a1-polish did. `brief.md` is the preamble, the rules and the skeleton with its slots; `reply_raw.json` is the untouched reply. The structure (nodes, jumps, flags, checks, who speaks) is the worker's; Gemini wrote every spoken line, Narrator line, player option and interject. Rows were filled into the skeleton by a script, then edited by hand as listed.

Asked: 57 lines and 14 options (the board's answers, the Narrator's planchette lines, the bites of cold, the closing clues, the find-my-son request). All 71 slots came back.

Edits made to the draft: "aclimbing" to "a climbing", "church-yard" to "churchyard"; the option "Who is the grey cat upstairs?" is "Who is the grey cat?" (the cat is met at the gate); "What is that big red dog on the third floor?" is "What is Shuck, the red dog?" (there is no dog on the third floor); the answer "SHE CRAVES THEIR SOULS. THIS HOUSE HUNTS FOR HER." is "SHE WANTS THEIR SOULS FOR THE HOUSE." (the draft had the house hunting the hag); "SHE IS THIS HOUSE." (Sally) is "SHE WAS A CHILD OF THIS HOUSE. SHE RUNS FREE NOW."; three Narrator lines said "The wood" for the planchette and now say "The pointer". The rest went in as drafted. Dropped: none.

Worker's own choices: the roll after each answer is `check Constitution DC 6` (the party's best Constitution; a stand-in for the book's 1 on a d10, about one failure in ten for a typical best bonus). Each failed roll is narration plus the counter `seance_stings` and a `damage <dice> psychic party` (1d4, 1d6, 1d8, 1d10, then 1d12 from the fifth sting; r12-statements). The question menu is eight questions that unlock by earlier answers and by `visited:` floors; "that is enough" asks once more before the board goes dead for good.
