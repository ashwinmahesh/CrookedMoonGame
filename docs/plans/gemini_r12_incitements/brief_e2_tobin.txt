THE COMPANION: TOBIN HAYWARD, a Harvestborn (living scarecrow, straw and burlap) Druid, a pragmatic rural diagnostician of curses and rot. Burlap thumbs, straw ribs, little field mice in his lapels, stiff pruning knife. Brisk and unsentimental, fussy, afraid of open flame (a spark would burn his straw and the dried wedding flowers braided in his chest). His secret (never state it): in a famine he burned a neighbour's blighted orchard to save his own.
HIS VOICE: brisk diagnostic sentences in tidy counted rhythms that stop on a dry two-beat pause; gentle farmhand's country drawl; orchard, grafting, blight, soil, timber and stitching words. Pet phrases: "tie off the rot" (once a scene at most), "good soil, bad seed", "hold still now". Calls the Crooked Man "the crooked spindle-man". Under stress his words crowd together. His existing lines (do not repeat): "Hold still, you're fraying at the cuff and your pulse is skipping every fourth beat." / "Keep that lantern back, two paces back, I am dry straw from collar to heel." / "Dirt round here tastes of sour turnip and third-rate hexing."

THE SCENE (the Conservatory, glass walls and glass roof, rain hammering, dead vines, benches, and one vivid living bergamot tree with hard green fruit; a woman in black sits still on a bench facing it). Tobin steps past the dead vines, presses a burlap thumb to the tree's weeping bark and jerks his hand back as if the sap scalded him. In the tree he sees black rot split the trunk and run out across the valley toward a hill crawling with pale centipedes, and a phantom smell of scorching apple-wood sets the mice squeaking and diving for his pockets. He pulls three straws from his ribs, twists a stiff splint round a cracked bergamot twig, and clears his throat hard to cover the shake in his fingers.
SIGNATURE LINE (use it verbatim as the FIRST INTERJECT of the start node): "Bark's cold, nothing burning here, only a deep sour canker running down the taproot toward that centipede hill."
NODE start: Narrator describes the thumb on the bark and the jerked-back hand and the weeping sap; the signature interject; Narrator describes the smell of scorching apple-wood, the mice, the splint and the shaking fingers; a second interject, Tobin steadying himself with a diagnosis.
THE THREE OPTIONS and what each means for Tobin:
opt1 "What did that tree just show you?" => he steadies and commits to tracking the rot to its source.
opt2 "Your hand shook. Did the sap burn you?" => he feels seen past the mask and admits, without detail, that the rot reminds him of an old orchard fire.
opt3 "Leave the tree. We have a manor to clear." => he pockets his pruning knife and keeps the diagnosis to himself; the quiet way on.
Each option node is the aftermath: the Narrator line shows what Tobin does with his hands (splint, mice, knife, straw); the interject is his voice answering the party.
