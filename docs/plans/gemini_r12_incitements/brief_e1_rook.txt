THE COMPANION: ROOK COLDWAKE, a Graveborn (ghoul-like undead) Fighter, a watchman who treats horrific nightmares as rude workplace infractions. Lantern-jawed, broad-shouldered, in frost-rimmed plate, with a coffin-lid tower shield that has a brass door-knocker on it. He takes chalk rubbings of headstones in a soggy leather ledger, judges ground by its drainage, forgets to breathe until reminded. His secret (never state it): he wears a stolen Rest Warden badge, the number on it scraped over with a knife; he carries a packet of blue cornflower seeds.
HIS VOICE: measured, clipped baritone, flat Northern English with a dry drawl, municipal and masonry words (subsidence, zoning, regulation, drainage, docket), deadpan, level sentences like shift reports. Pet phrases: "Mind the turf", "Settle down now", "past regulation", "on the shift log". Under stress his cadence goes stiff and cold and protective. His existing lines (do not repeat): "Ground here has decent drainage, good firm clay under the moss, though the headstones are leaning five degrees past regulation." / "Technically I stopped breathing three parishes back, so if someone reminds me to sigh before supper, I can check it off the shift log." / "My fingers aren't taking the chill right anymore, they feel soft, like river mud." / "That grave was consecrated, sealed, and paid for in full."

THE SCENE (the Parlour, a dusty room with a round table, a spirit board, a cobwebbed fireplace and a crystal ball on a shelf; rain on the shuttered windows). The crystal ball fills with pale, churning frost instead of smoke, and tiny faces press out against the glass, mouthing words. The brass knocker on Rook's shield clacks once by itself. He opens his ledger past pages of grey rubbings to three blank sheets at the back, and thumbs his badge. The spirits in the glass are showing the valley's souls being corrupted and needing cleansing, but never say that flatly; the scene is the frost and the faces.
SIGNATURE LINE (use it verbatim as the FIRST INTERJECT of the start node): "Gross neglect of parish bounds, that is, and three districts without proper stone or seal. Settle down now, we've a docket to clear."
NODE start: Narrator describes the frost and the faces; the signature interject; Narrator describes the knocker clacking and the ledger's blank pages and the badge; a second interject, Rook muttering to himself or the glass.
