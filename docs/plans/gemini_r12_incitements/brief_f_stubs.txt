These five scenes are for the party's custom hero, a player-made character, never a named companion. The hero speaks in the FIRST PERSON as the player's own voice; there are NO Narrator lines at all, and no name appears anywhere. Each scene is a short moment of the hero meeting a sign of their own personal story inside the Crooked House. The first-person lines are plain, specific, a little haunted, two sentences at most, under about 30 words each. They must make sense for any gender and any species. Sometimes use a concrete sensory detail; never explain a secret flatly; leave the hook open. Do not invent new people, places or monsters beyond the facts given. Plain punctuation only; no em dashes, no semicolons, no brackets, no backticks.

OUTPUT FORMAT for each scene, nothing else:

SCENE duality
NODE start
PLAYER: the line
PLAYER: the line

NODE opt1
PLAYER: the line
PLAYER: the line
INSPIRE: four to seven words, past tense, no name, saying what the hero did

(then opt2 and opt3.) The start node has exactly two PLAYER rows. Each option node has exactly two PLAYER rows then one INSPIRE row. Also give the three OPTIONS texts at the top of each scene, one per line, as OPTION1: ... OPTION2: ... OPTION3: ... each under 12 words, spoken by the hero or the party, working at any hour. The third option is always a quiet way on that still completes the scene.

SCENE duality. Place: the Music Room. The hero's story: a part of them was split off long ago, taking their reflection and shadow, and now wanders the world as a double that looks just like them and commits crimes in their name. The moment: in the cracked oval mirror the hero's reflection grins when they do not, holds a bloody axe before a field of corpses, and says that sin of every kind feeds it. The options: speak to the reflection / smash the glass / turn away.
SCENE immortality. Place: the cauldron room in the attic, a round window, a floor of teeth, a great cauldron of glowing red liquid. The hero's story: they studied blood alchemy and carry a flask holding a being that has promised them first a cure and then immortality. The moment: the being in the flask goes still and then eager, recognising the pale soul-glyphs cut into the floor around the cauldron, and shows the hero how to etch the same glyphs on its vessel; the next step, it says, is blood. The options: etch the glyphs on the flask / ask the flask what it knows / cork it and move on.
SCENE kindred. Place: the master bedroom. The hero's story: a relative of theirs vanished after dreaming of the Crooked Man, a tall lanky hanged man in a bent hat with a huge yellow grin. The moment: scratched in the plaster behind the bed are the nightmares of whoever slept here, a long list in the same cramped hand; the last scratch speaks of centipedes in the deep. The options: read the scratchings aloud / trace the last scratch / leave it for now.
SCENE malediction. Place: the Library. The hero's story: a curse runs in their bloodline and only occult lore can break it; they carry a stolen page from a book. The moment: on a stand lies a heavy old tome that is half written and half blank, and the torn edge of one page matches the page the hero carries; in the margins are notes about demons that collect secrets, eyes and wings. The options: lay the stolen page against the tome / read the margin notes / close the tome and leave.
SCENE rapture. Place: the Gallery. The hero's story: they belong to a secret society that serves an otherworldly power and are a prophet of a god who seeks a foothold in a godless land. The moment: a plaque under a dour marble bust names a missionary who gathered followers to bring a god into the Hollow, and the hero feels the cold marble answer their faith. The options: read the plaque aloud / pray before the bust / walk on.
