THE COMPANION: LUCAN HOUNDSTOOTH, a Curseborn (wolfen) Ranger, a courtly, solemn gentleman hunter in a stolen legend of a coat, afraid that the beast in his blood is a coward. His spectral hound Dreadfang is really the ghost of his childhood lapdog, a small Pomeranian called Barnaby, who puffs himself up into a nightmare mastiff so his master need not walk the woods alone. Lucan grades everything in a field journal. His secret (never state it): he froze when a beast killed his hunting partner and took the credit.
HIS VOICE: measured, rolling sentences with polite subordinate clauses; soft-spoken mountain drawl; almost no contractions; Victorian naturalist and field-sport vocabulary, taxonomy, grades out of ten. Pet phrases: "if you please" (once a scene at most), "frankly dreadful", "hush now". Calls the Crooked Man "that poorly posture-trained gentleman". Embarrassment makes him stammer politely. His existing lines (do not repeat): "Hush now, the dark has teeth in this thicket, though by the look of those tracks, its posture is frankly dreadful." / "I awarded the bog-hag a generous six out of ten for ambush presentation." / "Prime specimen, dreadful form, take the hind leg!"

THE SCENE (the Trophy Room: stuffed beasts in absurd theatrical poses round a cold fireplace on the north wall, glass eyes like polished marbles, a very large brown bear on a plinth). Lucan sniffs the cedar-and-arsenic air, opens his field journal to grade the taxidermy, and freezes: on a low shelf a small stuffed Pomeranian, no bigger than a boot, has turned its head. Barnaby pads out from behind Lucan's boots and steps into the mount like a hand into a glove, and the little dog points its nose past the room at a stuffed black crow on a high shelf, whose feathers smell faintly of burnt straw. Barnaby then yips at a crumb of cheddar on the bear's plinth, and for the first time Lucan does not call it an omen. No animal is harmed; the mount is only a coat for the ghost. Never mention an attic or trapdoor.
SIGNATURE LINE (use it verbatim as the FIRST INTERJECT of the start node): "Pay no mind to the cheddar, friends. Dreadfang has scented a prime specimen in that crow, and I give the taxidermist four out of ten for the beak."
NODE start: Narrator describes the stuffed beasts, the cedar-and-arsenic air and the turned Pomeranian head; the signature interject; Narrator describes Barnaby stepping into the mount, pointing at the crow with its burnt-straw smell, and yipping at the crumb; a second interject, Lucan covering his flustered dignity.
THE THREE OPTIONS and what each means for Lucan:
opt1 "What's your hound pointing at, Lucan?" => he treats the party as colleagues of the hunt and shares his reading of the crow.
opt2 "Did your omen of death just beg for cheese?" => he bristles with flustered dignity and doubles down on his persona.
opt3 "Leave the mounts. We've a house to clear." => he marks the crow in his field journal alone, grateful not to be inspected; the quiet way on.
Each option node is the aftermath: the Narrator line shows what Lucan does with his hands (journal, pencil, collar, the small dog); the interject is his voice answering the party.
