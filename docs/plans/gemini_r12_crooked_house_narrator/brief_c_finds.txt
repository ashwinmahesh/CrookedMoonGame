BATCH C: FINDS, MISSES, TRAPS AND CONTAINERS. One block per key, PLAIN only. A "search" line is the one sentence that plays at the moment a hidden thing is found. A "miss" line plays when a hero searches and finds nothing but a single small human tooth, which they pick up: the house leaves teeth where treasure should be. Every miss line must end with a tooth in the hand, be one short sentence (two at most), say a different thing each time (a different place the tooth is wedged or lies, a different colour or condition of the tooth), and never feel like a dead end or a joke. Trap lines: "found" is the tell that gave the trap away (one sentence); "triggered" is the moment of impact, not the damage (one sentence).

KEY trap:h1_porch_edge:found
Facts: the boards at the very edge of the porch are scorched black in ragged lines, and the air there smells of struck iron.

KEY trap:h1_porch_edge:triggered
Facts: a hero steps off the edge of the porch into the storm. Lightning and thunder strike together and throw them back onto the boards.

KEY trap:balcony_edge:found
Facts: along the balcony rail the paint is blistered and the wood split, as if by lightning, and the rain hisses where it lands.

KEY trap:balcony_edge:triggered
Facts: a hero climbs or leans out past the balcony rail into the storm. The sky cracks white and the thunder drives them back onto the boards.

KEY trap:h7_oven_trap:found

KEY trap:h7_oven_trap:triggered
