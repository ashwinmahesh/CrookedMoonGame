BATCH D: THE RETRY LINES. When the whole party falls in a fight in the Crooked House, the Vagrant stops his own telling, puts the party back on its feet and starts the fight again from where they entered that floor. This is the Vagrant interrupting himself, quietly, in this house's hushed, dry, dead-level voice: no joke, no scolding, no gloating, not harsh and not cheerful. Warm underneath, steady. Each line says, in the story's own terms, that the tale did not end there and that he will tell that part over. Never use the words retry, restart, game, save, again-and-again, dead, die, defeat or lose. Do not describe the fall itself. Each line is the same kind of thing as: "Now wait just a minute, friends, that isn't how this part goes. Let me back the song up to where you stepped in." Do not copy that wording; vary every line.

For each key give PLAIN and PLAIN2 (two different plain lines), each one or two sentences, under about 28 words. Each line may mention the fight's own thing in a few words (the stone bird, the tune, the table, the chiming clock, the shadow in the nursery cupboard and so on) but must not name a monster. Where the key says how the party comes back, work that in once or twice across the whole batch, not in every line.
