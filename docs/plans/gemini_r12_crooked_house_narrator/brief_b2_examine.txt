BATCH B2: EXAMINE LINES (bathroom to the attic). One block per key, PLAIN only unless an EXTRA is named. Each line says what the thing is and what it implies; the second sentence is the payoff or the unease. Where a key says "the fight begins", the line is the last quiet look before trouble, so tilt toward it without naming a monster or saying what happens next. Never give away who is really who in the cauldron room.

KEY examine:h11_tub
Facts: a claw-foot tub stained red to the rim, warm water or something like it still, steam on the mouldy tile, the smell of copper. The fight begins when you lean over it, so end on the surface of the water moving.

KEY examine:h11_door
Facts: the bathroom door, a trapezoid that hardly fits its frame, swollen wood, a tarnished knob. EXTRA [class:rogue]: starts with {name}, about the lock.

KEY examine:h12_bed
Facts: a canopy bed, curtains torn to ribbons, covers heaped and heaving as if something sleeps badly beneath them. Small white teeth lie scattered through the sheets. The fight begins when you pull the covers back, so end on the heaving.

KEY examine:h12_journal
Facts: a black leather journal on the desk, gilt monogram worn almost smooth, a small brass clasp. It is locked.

KEY examine:h12_balcony_doors
Facts: double doors of black glass at the end of the room, a brass catch crusted green, rain running down the other side.

KEY examine:h12_scratchings
Facts: long gouges in the plaster behind the bed, in bunches of four, as if by fingernails. Some are far too low down for a grown hand.

KEY examine:h13_crib
Facts: a mahogany crib, the rails riddled with tiny holes like pinpricks, a small threadbare blue teddy bear in the corner of the mattress, stuffed toys on a shelf above. The fight begins when you take the bear, so end on a toy's eye catching the light.

KEY examine:h14_cart
Facts: a liquor cart under cobweb, decanters furred with dust, and on the lower shelf four small stoppered vials that are strangely clean.

KEY examine:h14_tome
Facts: a heavy tome on a stand, cracked leather, the page edges brown, pages half covered in cramped ink and half empty.

KEY examine:h14_desk
Facts: a writing desk with a tall stack of books on it, a dry inkwell, a pen gone stiff, deep claw marks in the leather top.

KEY examine:h15_trapdoor
Facts: a square seam in the ceiling plaster with an iron ring set in it, grey with age. The air near it hums faintly. Touching it starts a roar that you feel in your teeth, so end on the first low note.

KEY examine:h15_bear
Facts: a very large brown bear mounted on its hind legs, claws raised, glass eyes. The seams along its chest and throat are stitched finer than any other mount in the room.

KEY examine:h15_trophy_crow
Facts: a stuffed black crow on a high shelf, wings half spread, one glass eye catching your light, dull feathers under dust.

KEY examine:h16_runes
Facts: runes cut deep into the walls, floor and ceiling, glowing blood-red and pulsing slowly. Six empty circles are cut among them, each about the size of a hand.

KEY examine:h17_cauldron
Facts: a great iron cauldron of glowing red liquid, steaming. Where it has slopped over the lip the floor has been eaten into pits. Teeth lie thick around its feet.

KEY examine:h17_phillip
Facts: a man in a fine suit bound beside the cauldron, head lolling, feverish, breathing shallowly.

KEY examine:h17_adela
Facts: a cloaked figure, hood forward, swaying on the spot like a reed, taking no step and making no sound.

KEY examine:h17_scratchings
Facts: scratchings on the wall, crude letters cut in a cramped furious hand, two names repeated over and over. Do not give the names.
