BATCH B1: EXAMINE LINES (first floor, second floor and the children's room). One block per key, PLAIN only unless an EXTRA is named. Each line says what the thing is and what it implies; the second sentence is the payoff or the unease. Where a key says "the fight begins", the line is the last quiet look before trouble, so let the second sentence tilt toward it without naming a monster or saying what happens next.

KEY examine:h1_porch_edge
Facts: where the porch boards end at the rail, with the storm beyond it. Rain slants past the lip, lightning shows only black water falling, and the thunder is overhead.

KEY examine:h2_chandelier
Facts: twenty feet up, hanging tilted, half its crystals gone, thick with dust and cobweb. It sways a little though no draught moves.

KEY examine:h2_stair
Facts: a curved stair climbing to a balcony. The treads are different heights, the runner is bruised purple and worn through, the banister leans.

KEY examine:h3_fireplace
Facts: a cold hearth under cobweb, soot-black, with a mantel of dusty ornaments. A faint smell of old ash. Nothing burns.

KEY examine:h3_crystal_ball
Facts: a glass ball on a brass claw stand on a shelf. It is clouded and cold, and your light does not reach its heart.

KEY examine:h4_peacock
Facts: an oversized albino peacock of white marble in an alcove, tail fanned in stone, one eye a dull red stone. The fight begins when you reach for the red stone, so end on a faint tick or shift in the stone.

KEY examine:h4_bust
Facts: a dour marble bust on a plinth, lips turned down, a small brass plaque below it gone dark with tarnish. Do not say what the plaque says.

KEY examine:h5_mirror
Facts: a tall cracked oval mirror, the silver gone black in spots, one long crack from edge to edge. Your own faces look back, a hair out of step.

KEY examine:h6_table
Facts: the long table and its feast: a sugar bowl, a roast fish, a lobster claw, a pastry, a beef tongue, steam rising straight up. The cloth is spotless though dust lies thick on everything else. The smell is sweet rot. The fight begins when you step to the table, so end on the steam or a plate stirring.

KEY examine:h7_shelf
Facts: a tall metal shelf fallen across the floor, tins and jars burst and rusted, the contents long since turned to black crust. Nothing here is usable.

KEY examine:h8_bergamot
Facts: the one living tree in the glass room, glossy leaves and hard green fruit, among dead brown vines. The bark looks damp, and a sharp citrus smell cuts through the mildew. The rain hammers the glass roof above it.

KEY examine:h8_cup
Facts: a porcelain cup, thin as an eggshell with a hair-line gold rim, on a saucer. The tea in it is dark and steams, though nothing in the room is warm.

KEY examine:h9_clock
Facts: a grandfather clock, case leaning away from the wall, glass door cracked, brass pendulum swinging. The tick is slow and louder than it ought to be, and the hands read no hour that makes sense. The fight begins as you open it, so end on something small stirring inside the case. EXTRA [class:wizard]: starts with {name}.

KEY examine:h9_rail
Facts: the cracked rail of the sitting room, looking down over the foyer twenty feet below. A split in the wood, the portraits' eyes turned up toward you, the tilted chandelier at eye level.

KEY examine:h10_closet
Facts: a closet door standing ajar, small coats and a school cap hanging in the dark, a smell of old wool.

KEY examine:h10_bed_left
Facts: the left of two small beds beside the dark window, blanket rumpled, a deep dust in the dip of the pillow, the dark beneath the bed deeper than it should be.

KEY examine:h10_bed_right
Facts: the right of two small beds beside the dark window, blanket pulled tight and square, a single cloth doll's shoe on the pillow, the dark beneath the bed deeper than it should be. (The doll's shoe is a real detail; no doll is visible.)

KEY examine:h10_chest
Facts: a big toy chest with a heavy lid, painted animals flaking off the sides, a brass hasp. Something inside shifts a little each time the house creaks.

KEY examine:h10_armoire
Facts: a child's armoire carved with stars, its two doors not quite meeting, a thin line of black between them.

KEY examine:h10_window
Facts: a small window sealed shut, black glass streaked with rain, lightning making your own reflections jump. There is no outside to see.
