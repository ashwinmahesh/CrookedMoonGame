## Camp talks

Three personal talks, drafted by Gemini (briefs `Gemini/brief_B_delphine.txt`, raw replies `Gemini/reply_B_delphine.json`) and converted by Lane F into the camp-file format: the companion's lines as `interject name:delphine:`, long lines split, approvals and flags added (the edit log is in `Gemini/README.md`). They are offered at a camp or after a Long Rest, each plays once, and each takes one of the companion's two words a day; they come after any Strained or Doubtful talk and before the Warm, Close and Devoted talks.

- **Unlocks.** Talk 1 `flag.connection_ascendancy`. Talk 2 `flag.connection_ascendancy`, the next word after talk 1; it is where `secret_known_delphine` is set, at Warm approval or better, which is what lets another hero pay Delphine's dark secret at Raum's gate. Talk 3 `flag.ascendancy_fool_reborn`.
- **The talks.**
  1. The notebook: she has cracked the Fool's blueprint; the turn is a loose page of crossed-out nursery rhymes.
  2. Buttons: a beast-tooth button on her coat; the turn is that she is sure she will spill into sawdust if she stops mending.
  3. The waltz: a crooked carnival step; the turn is that she never learned how the song ends.
- **Rules kept.** No roll or check anywhere; every menu has the kind exit "Not now. Rest easy, Delphine."; the dark secret is never stated in a camp talk, only approached.
