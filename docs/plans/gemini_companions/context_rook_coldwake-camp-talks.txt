## Camp talks

Three personal talks, drafted by Gemini (briefs `Gemini/brief_B_rook.txt`, raw replies `Gemini/reply_B_rook.json`) and converted by Lane F into the camp-file format: the companion's lines as `interject name:rook:`, long lines split, approvals and flags added (the edit log is in `Gemini/README.md`). They are offered at a camp or after a Long Rest, each plays once, and each takes one of the companion's two words a day; they come after any Strained or Doubtful talk and before the Warm, Close and Devoted talks.

- **Unlocks.** Talk 1 `flag.connection_pilgrimage`. Talk 2 `flag.connection_pilgrimage`, the next word after talk 1; it is where `secret_known_rook` is set, at Warm approval or better, which is what lets another hero pay Rook's dark secret at Raum's gate. Talk 3 `flag.pilgrimage_confronted`.
- **The talks.**
  1. The ledger: rubbings and headstone lettering; the crack is that he cannot call up his own mother's handwriting.
  2. The knocker: the code the buried tapped on coffin wood; the turn is the knocks you can answer and the ones you can't dig out.
  3. The Deep Thaw: do his knuckles look grey; his practical instructions for the cairn if he turns.
- **Rules kept.** No roll or check anywhere; every menu has the kind exit "Not now. Rest easy, Rook."; the dark secret is never stated in a camp talk, only approached.
