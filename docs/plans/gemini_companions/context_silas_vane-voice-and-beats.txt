VOICE SHEET: SILAS VANE.
Male, apparent age 24, a Relicborn (skeleton-folk, green pinpoints of light in his eye sockets) monk and street hustler from Kalero's river landings. Timbre: lean, wiry, bright, with a youthful dockside rasp like fine sandpaper over brass; quick intakes of breath between phrases. Pace: rapid-fire, syncopated patter, accelerating through setups and snapping shut on the punchline or the tell. Accent: urban estuary and dockside British lilt, dropped H's, nimble melodic rises. Temperament: cocky, observant, kinetic when calm, treating horrors as marks at a card table. When rattled he talks faster and higher, until a rare quiet truth slips out flat and bare. He uses gaming and gambling words (tell, stake, house, marked, shoe, fold, double down, tab, the fine print). Pet phrases: "House always wins, unless you burn the house" and "Tell's in the twitch, friend." He calls people "friend". He must never sound like a middle-aged gangster, a clattery spooky-cartoon skeleton, a moustache-twirling villain or a Victorian pickpocket cliché.

Nobody leaves the party in this Thread: his shadow becomes a person, and he rides the Last Ride.

## The one idea

A rigged debt can never be paid by letting someone else pick up the tab. Silas treats every horror as a crooked game with a tell because admitting the game is unwinnable means facing what he did to survive. His creditor, **the Dealer**, holds his contract; years ago, at a crossroads in Kalero, Silas palmed the marked card so that his younger brother drew the black spot instead. His brass censer is the Dealer's eye on him, packed with bitter wormwood to blind it, and his own smaller shadow lags a half-step behind him as the Dealer's silent lookout. Across six beats Silas moves from hunting a cheap loophole to turning his spying shadow into a partner, then to the one hand he cannot cheat.

People invented for this Thread:
- **Wick Vane** (Gemini named him Pip; renamed so he is not confused with Delphine's old draft name), Silas's wide-eyed younger brother, a river-landing lookout in Kalero who trusted Silas's shuffle and was dragged into the Dealer's ledger in his place. Fourteen when taken.
- **Mags Nickel**, a toothless river-landing fence who taught Silas to shave the edges of bone tarot cards and died owing the Dealer three winters.
- **Osric Quill** (Gemini named him Barnaby; renamed because Lucan's ghost dog is Barnaby), a drowned river-clerk whose warning letter turns up in the ghost train's mailbags.
- **Chalk**, Silas's shadow once the contract is broken: a mute, grinning little skeleton shade who rubs out his tally marks.

## The six beats

### 1. Incitement: the letter in the mailbags

- **Where and when.** The Mariner's car (G5) on the Ghostlight Express (R10), the first time Silas is in the party when the party enters it. The old sailor's ghost heaves mailbags across the floor.
- **Prerequisite.** None.
- **The player sees.** Silas feels a chill rattle through his ribs and freezes at burnt sealing-wax and river-landing silt on the draught. A brass-cornered envelope slips from a torn sack and lands face up at his boots, sealed with a grinning skull-and-spade mark. Inside, the parchment shows no ink until his lagging shadow leans over it: Osric Quill's cramped warning about a house game at the Drowned Crossroads, where a soul can be won back across the baize if the player's own shadow is willing to cheat, and a hand-drawn diagram of a dealer's table with two empty chairs. Silas stuffs a pinch of wormwood into his censer until green smoke fills the car and hides the page from the eye inside the brass.
- **Choices:**
  - "Show us what's on that paper, Silas." He shows the two empty chairs and admits he owes for more than himself.
  - "Burn the letter before the smoke clears." He touches his flint-lighter to the corner, keeping his hand close to his waistcoat.
  - "Pocket it and keep moving." He slips it into his bone deck with a crooked grin.
- **Sets.** `incitement_liberation`; `silas_inc_choice` = `shown` / `burned` / `pocketed` (Holly's greeting reads it). The shadow begins mimicking card-dealing whenever he stands near a lantern.
- **Reward.** Heroic Inspiration to Silas.
- **If benched.** Pending (`pending_liberation_incitement`). The Mariner's car is re-enterable until the crash and the letter stays in its sack; after the crash the beat plays as the echo at the next camp.
- **Signature line.** "Taste that on the wind, friend, river-tar and scorched tallow through the teeth, which means my old landlord just slid a bill under the door and expects me to fold."

### 2. Connection: Holly Aster

- **Where and when.** Bread by Holly (V14), on the Founder's Round's north side, open from early Twilight to the start of Nightfall. The trigger is the first time the party passes the shop door during those hours after the Crooked House is cleansed, with Silas in the party.
- **Prerequisite.** `incitement_liberation`.
- **The player sees.** The shop is thick with toasted caraway and woodsmoke, with a cold draught of grave-mould that sets Silas's jaw clicking. Violet and Lavender chase flour-moths round the cooling racks. Holly wipes her hands on her apron and passes Silas a warm loaf; a grey birthmark shaped like a spade pip shows on her wrist. Silas's shadow reaches across the counter by itself and taps a chalk-white fingerbone against it, matching it to a tally mark inside his own ribs. Holly goes rigid, then whispers behind the oven's roar that an unseen creditor holds the deed to her thirty-fifth birthday, and that the only ledger office she knows of floats on a neon riverboat at the Drowned Crossroads. She presses a pinch of blessed hearth-salt into his palm.
- **Choices:**
  - "We'll tear your page out too." Silas swears a gambler's oath on his deck.
  - "What did the creditor take for it?" Silas trades a hard truth about how the Dealer baits family traps.
  - "Take the loaf. Leave her in peace." Silas backs out and adds one more mark to the inside of his ribs.
- **Sets.** `connection_liberation`; `attitude holly_aster friendly`; `liberation_holly_page_seen` (the Aster line is in the Dealer's ledger).
- **Reward.** Holly is Friendly and Silas has a day's hearth-blessing (Bless). A caraway roll that stays warm in his pack until the Prize Counter.
- **If benched.** The trigger still lets Holly be a hub scene; the Connection is pending with a marker on V14, and she greets him by name when he next walks in.
- **Signature line.** "Fresh rye and cold iron, lovely. The baker's playing against the same rigged shoe I am, and the Dealer's already dealt her the dead man's hand."

### 3. Discovery: the hexed table

- **Where and when.** The Dead Man's Hand (R15), the gambling hall on the main deck, the first time Silas is in the party when the party walks in. The riverboat's passage requires each hero to have taken one thing from Mister Crossroads's shack; the beat plays whichever thing it was.
- **Prerequisite.** `connection_liberation`.
- **The player sees.** Green neon buzzes over the five-skeleton band's horn solo and the clatter of enchanted horse figurines. Silas stalks past the roulette wheels to a green baize table crewed by a hollow-eyed undead dealer. A deck of bone-sliver cards hums in a silver shoe like locust wings, and the top card flips itself to show Wick Vane behind bars of black spade-pips, a dealer's chalk tallying his ribs. Silas's censer jerks on its chain as the Dealer tries to see through the smoke. His shadow detaches its hands from the carpet and, with a silent, comic bow, slips a phantom card from the bottom of the shoe into his sleeve. The croupier rasps that only a Wild Joker will make the ledger's master sit down for a straight draw, and those are sold at fairs that take a whole town.
- **Choices:**
  - "Let your shadow deal the next hand." Silas laughs and partners with his former spy, teaching it three dockside sleights.
  - "Where does the Dealer keep the boy?" Silas slams a bony hand on the moving card and the croupier rattles out the rules of a blood buyout.
  - "Scoop the cards and walk away." Silas pockets the palmed card and leaves before the ferrymen call the house.
- **Sets.** `discovery_liberation`; `liberation_shadow_ally` (the shadow now answers his hand signals); `liberation_wick_seen` (the party has seen the card).
- **Reward.** One Twist of Fate.
- **If benched.** Pending, with the riverboat staying moored in the lagoon until the Sinner falls; after that the gambling hall is gone and the beat converts to its echo at the next camp. The last-door reminder (the boarding of the Dead Man's Hand) names any companion with an unplayed beat.
- **Signature line.** "Look at the little shade go, palming aces off the bottom like a proper river-rat, and all we need now is a clown's trump to break the bank."

### 4. Confrontation: the Wild Joker

- **Where and when.** Fool's Day (R21), the Prize Counter (C3) run by Chuckles, the first time the party reaches the counter with Silas in the party.
- **Prerequisite.** `discovery_liberation`.
- **The player sees.** Sour candy-floss smoke and paper streamers; Chuckles cackles behind a glass case holding a gleaming foil Wild Joker marked at infinity tickets. Silas distracts him by juggling his smoking censer and three carnival stubs, while his shadow stretches ten feet across the sawdust, picks the case's lock with its own fingerbone, and slides the card into his waistcoat. The instant the foil touches his deck the painted jester on it grins with the Dealer's voice, leaving a taste of old copper and canal water on the air, and offers to put Wick's and the Aster line's pages on the table once the Crooked Queen falls, if Silas wagers the empty throne of Druskenvald itself.
- **Choices:**
  - "Accept the wager. Name the time." Silas taps the card with a chalked claw and doubles down.
  - "Spit on it. We bring our own deck." He refuses the card the satisfaction of a fair agreement.
  - "Shove it in the censer. Move on." He chokes the talking card in wormwood smoke until the game begins.
- **Sets.** `liberation_confronted`, `liberation_wild_joker` (the card is in his deck); `liberation_holly_ready` (Holly's page is now one he can win back; this feeds the Rowan's Rise redemption rule, see the overview).
- **Reward.** +2 to one ability (default Dexterity; the player may redirect; maximum 24).
- **If benched.** Pending. The festival state closes when Chuckles dies; the card stays in its case until Silas is active (the Prize Counter is part of the festival state) and, after the festival, the beat converts to its echo at the next camp. The last-door reminder (the Ring of Fools) names any companion with an unplayed beat.
- **Signature line.** "Infinity tickets is a steep buy-in, friend. Good thing my flat little partner has five sticky fingers and no respect for carnival arithmetic."

### 5. Climax: the last hand

- **Where and when.** The Wytchwood clearing (R24), after the skull ritual is resolved and the Crooked Tree has burned. Silas in the party. If Silas does not have the Wild Joker (`liberation_wild_joker` false because the beat was an echo that gave it to him), the card appears from his waistcoat all the same.
- **Prerequisite.** `liberation_confronted`. If Silas offered his Thread at the skull, this beat does not play.
- **The player sees.** White ash drifts; the Crooked Tree is embers; the Crooked Queen is bound in the moon above. The Wild Joker leaps from Silas's waistcoat and deals three giant cards of black smoke onto a stump. A white-gloved hand with no arm behind it rises from the middle card and holds open a ledger: Silas's page, Wick Vane's page with a living portrait mouthing a silent warning, and the Aster line's page with Violet's and Lavender's names under it. The Dealer wants the lordship of Druskenvald for the stake. Silas plays three plays, no dice, each named for something he has learned to read in the Dealer: the Shave (marked edges, from Mags Nickel), the Hearth-Cut (a cut that looks fair, from Holly's oven), the Bottom-Deal (the shadow's trick, which the Dealer plays too). He cannot lose a play; what differs is the price. On the final turn the hand's thumb twitches over Wick's page.
- **Choices** (the book's A and B, personalised):
  - **A**, the bigger price: "Call the twitch. Play it straight." The shadow, Chalk, steps off the floor and sits out the last hand, so Silas plays it clean; he gives up cheating for good. He reads the tell, wins, and the ledger dissolves; the throne stays empty and Wick, Silas and the Aster line go free, with certainty.
  - **B**: "Burn the house." He smashes the censer through the Joker and sets the ledger alight from the Tree's embers. The Dealer's hold breaks by force and every page in it burns, the honest debts with the rest. Silas will never know for certain what became of Wick's page.
  - Quiet way on: "Your play, Silas. We're with you." Silas decides, and plays it straight (resolves as A).
- **Sets.** `climax_liberation`; `thread_liberation_catharsis` = `taken` (A) or `declined` (B); `liberation_chalk_born` (true in both); `liberation_burned_the_house` (B).
- **Reward.** A feat: **Lucky** (alternate Alert).
- **If benched.** The clearing stays open after the burn as the epilogue's "Wytchwood ash" place; the Last Ride prompt names Silas's unfinished hand. If the player boards anyway, the quiet Catharsis plays.
- **If he offered his Thread at the skull.** No Climax. The Wild Joker stays in his deck; at the epilogue the censer keeps swinging and Chalk keeps lagging a half-step behind.
- **If he gave his life at the skull (the Last Act).** The slide shows a velvet cuff wiping a chalk mark off a station post, and a small shadow walking away on its own.
- **Signature line.** A: "Tell's in the twitch, friend. You always crook your thumb before a stacked draw, so hand over the boy's page and choke on your new throne." B: "House always wins, unless you burn the house, smash the lamp and scatter the ledger across the cinders."

### 6. Catharsis: the honest twos

- **Where and when.** The platform in the fog (R25), as the Ghostlight Express arrives, in the epilogue's last scene before the Last Ride. Plays by the Climax flag.
- **The player sees.** Silas sits on a crate with no censer on his belt and the glow-beetle jar warming his knee. Chalk sits beside him in a cut-down waistcoat, dealing bone tarot cards that now show only painted ink, wiping a tally off the inside of Silas's ribs with a velvet cuff.
  - **If A (`taken`).** The top card flips to a calm, ordinary sketch of Wick Vane tossing a coin on a sunlit Kalero river landing. Violet and Lavender stand on the station steps in their best frocks, the grey spade mark gone from their family line, holding the hand of Holly if she turned at the stones, and otherwise of Siv Harga or Jean Myriel, who are minding them.
  - **If B (`declined`).** The top card flips to a coin in the air, still turning. Silas watches it for a long time and does not catch it.
- **Choices.** One: "What'll you play for now?" / "A toast to Wick and Chalk." / "Sit with him and watch the fog." All lead to the platform.
- **Sets.** `catharsis_liberation`.
- **Reward.** None. Chalk comes aboard.
- **If benched.** If Silas is not in the party at the epilogue, this beat plays anyway as a short platform scene; benching never takes the Catharsis.
- **Signature line.** "No smoke in the pot and no eye in the brass, friend, just a pair of honest twos and all the time in the world to lose them."

## The dark secret (Raum's gate, R13)

- **The confession, if Silas pays it himself.** "The Dealer came for my tab at a crossroads in Kalero four winters back, and I knew which bone card had the black spot shaved into the rim. I dealt from the bottom of the shoe, so my little brother Wick drew the mark instead of me. He smiled and told me not to worry while the fog took him."
- **The Narrator.** "The gate swings open on silent hinges, smelling of river silt and a boy's cheap tallow candle, snuffed."
- **The proof.** A shaved bone tarot card, the Hanged Man, with a tiny nick in the lower left corner and "Wick, 14" scratched on the back with a brass pin.
- **If Silas pays it** (`paid_own_secret_silas`): Raum gains a hold on Silas for the maze (the book). His approval of the player rises by 2 (the only quiet truth he has ever said aloud in company).
- **If another hero pays it** (possible only when `secret_known_silas`, set by camp talk 2 at Warm approval or better): rumour in Foxwillow and Wickermoor (`rumour_silas_brother`), Silas's approval falls by 8, and the first camp talk afterwards is the cold one.
- **Reveal reactions.** Tobin, who barred a neighbour's door, says nothing; he walks Silas's watch that night, three paces off the fire, so neither keeps the night alone.
