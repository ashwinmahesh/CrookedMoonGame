You are a narrative designer adding companion reactions to the dialogue of a single-player, turn-based party RPG on the D&D 2024
rules (a perpetual-Halloween valley; folks talk with a country drawl). The player leads four of six companions. Today the
companions almost never react to what happens in the story's big conversations, and they never approve or disapprove of the
player's choices there. Fix that for the chapter whose dialogue files are attached. Reply with JSON only (no Markdown fence).

Attached context:
- companions.txt: the six companions' voice sheets, facts and existing lines (use only these facts), then what each one
  approves and disapproves of, with example sizes and reasons.
- chapter_<name>_<n>.txt: the chapter's dialogue files, in parts, each file starting with a line "=== FILE: <file name>".

## How the dialogue files work (read, don't change)

`~ name` starts a node. `speaker [mood]: text` is a spoken line, `Narrator: text` is narration. `* option text -> node` is a
choice the player picks; `-> node` jumps; `if`/`endif` gate lines; `set`, `quest`, `combat` etc. are statements.

## What to add

1. "approve": on REAL choices only (spare or kill, help or refuse, lie or tell the truth, take or leave, mercy or cruelty,
   respect or mock the dead, a deal with something dark). Name the node an option leads to (the node right after `->` on a `*`
   line), never a node every path runs through. Move one to three companions who would care, with signs that disagree where it
   fits (one approves, another disapproves). Sizes: 1 a manner or small kindness; 2 or 3 a clear value choice; 4 to 6 a big moral
   choice; 8 to 10 only for a betrayal or a climax. Give the reason as the companion would remember it, starting "You", past tense,
   under 14 words ("You let the frightened hound go free"). Cover every real choice in the files, typically 6 to 15 per chapter.
   Skip choices that are only "ask about X", "leave", skill checks, or shop and travel menus.
2. "asides": a companion's one-line remark dropped into a big conversation right after another character's line, which plays
   only if that companion is in the party. 6 to 16 words, never over 20; spoken, in their voice, reacting to the line just before
   it (a joke, a worry, a jab, a kindness). Two or three per big conversation, at most one per node, spread across all six
   companions about evenly (each companion at least twice in the chapter). Never on a line inside a node that opens with options
   only, never right before a `*` option line (put it before the options' lead-in line instead, or skip). Never reveal a
   companion's dark secret; never mention a companion's own story scenes; no game words (hit points, level, quest, roll, check).
   No new named people or places.
