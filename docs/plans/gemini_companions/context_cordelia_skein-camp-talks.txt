## Camp talks

Three personal talks, drafted by Gemini (briefs `Gemini/brief_B_cordelia.txt`, raw replies `Gemini/reply_B_cordelia.json`) and converted by Lane F into the camp-file format: the companion's lines as `interject name:cordelia:`, long lines split, approvals and flags added (the edit log is in `Gemini/README.md`). They are offered at a camp or after a Long Rest, each plays once, and each takes one of the companion's two words a day; they come after any Strained or Doubtful talk and before the Warm, Close and Devoted talks.

- **Unlocks.** Talk 1 `flag.connection_evolution`. Talk 2 `flag.connection_evolution`, the next word after talk 1; it is where `secret_known_cordelia` is set, at Warm approval or better, which is what lets another hero pay Cordelia's dark secret at Raum's gate. Talk 3 `flag.evolution_confronted`.
- **The talks.**
  1. Upside down: tea by one ankle-thread; the turn is that lying flat in the dark feels like a cocoon.
  2. The jar: a lecture on calibration; the turn is that she kept the heart beating after she outgrew his laboratory.
  3. Pulses: macabre teasing about the party's circulation; the turn is that she checks their pulses because she forgets how easily they stop.
- **Rules kept.** No roll or check anywhere; every menu has the kind exit "Not now. Rest easy, Cordelia."; the dark secret is never stated in a camp talk, only approached.
