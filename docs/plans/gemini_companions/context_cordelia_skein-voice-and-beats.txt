VOICE SHEET: CORDELIA SKEIN.
Female, apparent age 29, a spider-folk sorcerer and self-taught bio-arcanist from Nerukhet, with four extra arms folded like a waistcoat, six eyes and ruby-glass vials plugged into her thorax. Timbre: cool, silky and glass-clear, a crisp bite on plosives and a faint natural whispery sibilance that lingers on S; the smooth, unruffled polish of a Victorian lecture hall, eerily soothing when she speaks of something lethal. Pace: brisk and metronomic, clipping through anatomical terms without stumbling. Accent: cool, cultivated, lightly British-leaning academic speech, pristine diction. Temperament: brightly inquisitive, polite and clinically detached when calm, speaking of arterial spray the way one speaks of weather. Under stress her voice drops to an icy breathless whisper and turns hyper-precise. She uses anatomical and scientific words affectionately (viscosity, specimen, pathology, humours, thorax, assay, ratio, calibration). Pet phrases: "Fascinating pathology!" and "Do hold still, this will only tingle." She calls people "darling". She must never sound like a campy femme fatale, a hissing cartoon snake-monster, a bubbly modern lab nerd or a theatrical stage barker.

## The one idea

Whether perfection of form can ever balance a ledger of betrayal paid in sibling blood. Cordelia treats her unstable body as a peer-reviewed study because clinical detachment is the only wall between her waking mind and the dark caverns of Nerukhet. She chases the formula of a dead researcher she idolises, **Doctor Hespa Marlowe**, hoping it will silence her fear of reverting to a mindless brood-beast. She meets a tailor who clothes all eight of her limbs with quiet dignity instead of clinical curiosity. In the crematorium she learns that replacing weak flesh needs stardust, not blood. At the crater in the Crooked Nightmare the meteorite answers the preserved heart of her chief experimenter in its jar, and offers a synthesis that could multiply across seven empty shells. At the end she chooses between becoming an immortal vessel free of biological guilt and keeping her blood and working one slow drop at a time.

People invented for this Thread:
- **Doctor Hespa Marlowe** (Gemini wrote Vane-Morley; renamed so she is not confused with Silas Vane), a human natural philosopher whose smuggled treatise on transmutative humours taught Cordelia to study her own mutations. She died at Memory's Rest after losing her mind to the serum.
- **Provost Alaric Glass**, the chief experimenter who bought Cordelia's clutch in the caverns of Nerukhet, whose heart still beats in her lead-lined jar.
- **Sibil**, the smallest of Cordelia's seven clutch-mates, who tied a tiny knot of unbleached silk round Cordelia's lowest wrist before the iron crate closed.

## The six beats

### 1. Incitement: the medical bag

- **Where and when.** The Jailbird's car (G6) on the Ghostlight Express (R10), the first time Cordelia is in the party when the party enters it. The striped prisoner ghost dreams aloud of the steak and eggs he never got.
- **Prerequisite.** None.
- **The player sees.** Fog presses on the glass. Wedged under the ghost's iron ball and chain is a cracked leather medical bag that smells of camphor and dried clove. Cordelia's six eyes dilate with an audible click as her lower hands draw out a water-stained monograph titled *On the Final Stage*, signed by Doctor Hespa Marlowe. She traces a diagram of a six-chambered vessel in crimson silk-thread that hangs in the air and dissolves into faint red mist.
- **Choices:**
  - "Read the margins aloud. Let us hear her method." Cordelia feels respected as a colleague and shares her ambition to synthesise the Apex Humour.
  - "Check the bag for an address before the fog takes it." Cordelia turns her cold pragmatism to tracing the doctor to the valley.
  - "Pack the notes. We need to keep moving." Cordelia tucks the monograph into her coat alone, behind polite reserve.
- **Sets.** `incitement_evolution`; `cordelia_inc_choice` = `read` / `traced` / `packed` (Thomasin's greeting reads it). She gains Hespa's unfinished equations.
- **Reward.** Heroic Inspiration to Cordelia.
- **If benched.** The Jailbird's car is re-enterable until the crash; after that, pending with the echo at the next camp.
- **Signature line.** "Fascinating pathology. She mapped the exact cellular threshold where crimson humours stop decaying and become permanent architecture."

### 2. Connection: Thomasin Ardor

Cordelia holds very still, her commentary stalling, as the garment settles without pinching a single joint.

### 3. Discovery: the crematorium

- **Where and when.** The Crematorium (L18) of Memory's Rest Sanatorium on the Isle of Mourn (R17): the first time Cordelia is in the party when the party enters. Two big brick cremators, blackened; twelve morgue lockers each holding a corpse marked by the serum; furnace ash that glints purple. The matron, Lethica Nightborne, has built the hospital over healing pools.
- **Prerequisite.** `connection_evolution`.
- **The player sees.** The room smells of cold soot and the sweet, flat tang of the serum drifting from the lockers. Before the blackened brick cremator Cordelia rakes through furnace ash that glints purple until her claws lift a scorched brass spectacle frame and Hespa's last ciphered ledger. When she sets her lead-lined jar on the iron table the heart of Provost Alaric Glass gives a slow, muffled thump against the glass, calibrating the cipher's crimson ink. The final pages say that science and magic failed together until Hespa looked to fallen stardust to replace weak flesh altogether.
- **Choices:**
  - "Hespa lost her mind here. Don't follow her blindly." Cordelia faces her own terror of losing her intellect and vows to refine the stardust without the serum.
  - "The formula's intact. Her death doesn't void the data." Cordelia hardens her clinical shell, using numbers to bury grief.
  - "Close the ledger. We have what we came for." Cordelia pockets the spectacles and ledger and goes silent.
- **Sets.** `discovery_evolution`; `augury_stardust_crater` (the missing reagent is stardust from the meteorite in the nightmare).
- **Reward.** One Twist of Fate.
- **If benched.** Pending. The sanatorium stays open while the party is in the chapter and after the Widow falls it remains a place; the beat re-arms at the crematorium. The Narrator says once on leaving: "The cremator cools with a sound like someone clearing her throat."
- **Signature line.** "She forgot her own name at the end, yet her ratios for celestial silicate remain scrupulously, maddeningly exact."

### 4. Confrontation: the stardust

- **Where and when.** The Crooked Nightmare (R22), N4 the smoking crater: a cool summer night, a clean half-moon, across a small bay the lights of a big modern city, a smoking crater with a seven-foot meteorite pulsing in every colour including impossible ones, and a teenage Adela walking down into it. The party passes N4 on the way along the line of dreamscapes, so the beat cannot be walked past. Cordelia in the party.
- **Prerequisite.** `discovery_evolution`.
- **The player sees.** Cordelia steps to the crater's lip in her eight-sleeved waistcoat and holds out the lead-lined jar. The heart of Provost Alaric Glass suddenly hammers like a trapped moth and rings against the lead with a high metallic chime that syncs to the stone's light (the player hears the chime and sees the jar pulse). A mote of stardust dissolves into the ruby vial on her thorax and turns the crimson liquid to liquid opal: a fluid that could replace her unstable flesh and be multiplied into seven pristine vessels. Her four lower hands, unclasping the waistcoat's buttons, freeze as she counts seven empty places in the dust. She understands that the valley's crooked corruption would twist any shared synthesis until its source is ended.
- **Choices:**
  - "You're counting seven doses. Who are they for?" Cordelia admits that her sum is haunted by an old debt and steadies her hand to finish the valley's fight first.
  - "Test the stardust on yourself alone." Cordelia embraces the ambition of solitary perfection and locks her guilt behind theory.
  - "Step back. We still have this dream to deal with." Cordelia seals the ringing jar in her coat and turns back in brittle silence.
- **Sets.** `evolution_confronted`; `cordelia_stardust_sample` (she carries the sample out of the dream).
- **Reward.** +2 to one ability (default Charisma; the player may redirect; maximum 24).
- **If benched.** Pending. The nightmare is a demiplane that ends with the abomination, so the beat converts to its echo at the next camp. The last-door reminder (before N8's attic door) names any companion with an unplayed beat in this chapter.
- **Signature line.** "The dust divides by seven with zero remainder. Hold still, that ringing is only my calibration heart matching the star."

### 5. Climax: the synthesis

- **Where and when.** The Wytchwood clearing (R24), after the skull ritual is resolved and the Crooked Tree has burned. Cordelia in the party.
- **Prerequisite.** `evolution_confronted`. If Cordelia offered her Thread at the skull, this beat does not play.
- **The player sees.** Ash from the burned Crooked Tree settles over the clearing and the Crooked Queen hangs bound in the moon. Cordelia kneels in the roots, her waistcoat singed at the hems, and unstoppers her thorax vials over a prism of raw stardust. The crimson in her veins hisses as it meets the mineral light, ready either to vitrify her whole body into an inorganic vessel that seeds the Hollow with iridescent dust, or to be damped down into a slow, finite tincture she can carry away. The jar rings once, and goes quiet.
- **Choices** (the book's A and B, personalised):
  - **A**, the bigger price: "Become the vessel. Let the stardust remake the Hollow." She becomes wholly inorganic, free of her fear of reversion and her living guilt, and the stardust spreads across the valley.
  - **B**: "Keep your blood. Refine it one drop at a time." She stays partly flesh, accepts her fragile stability and takes her work on at a modest, accountable scale.
  - Quiet way on: "Your experiment, Cordelia. We'll witness it." She does the careful reaction and the choice resolves as B.
- **Sets.** `climax_evolution`; `thread_evolution_catharsis` = `taken` (A) or `declined` (B); `cordelia_jar_silenced` (the heart in the jar stops in A, beats on in B).
- **Reward.** A feat: **Crimson Ritualist** (alternate Resilient, Constitution).
- **If benched.** The clearing stays open after the burn as the epilogue's "Wytchwood ash" place; the Last Ride prompt names her unfinished synthesis. If the player boards anyway, the quiet Catharsis plays.
- **If she offered her Thread at the skull.** No Climax. The jar still beats, and on the platform she hangs upside down from the lamp-post by one thread, taking notes.
- **If she gave her life at the skull (the Last Act).** The slide shows a charcoal waistcoat folded on a stump, and seven small silk cocoons drifting out over the valley.
- **Signature line.** "The reaction is complete. My pulse is now an equation that cannot sour, and I no longer require the dark."

### 6. Catharsis: seven

- **Where and when.** The platform at the village's edge (R25), as the Express arrives, in the epilogue's last scene. Plays by the Climax flag.
- **The player sees.** Lantern light gleams on the rails. Cordelia stands in Thomasin's waistcoat, spinning a web of crimson silk between her fingers that holds seven tiny, empty silk cocoons in a row. Instead of writing notes on them she lets the thread go slack.
  - **If A (`taken`).** The seven cocoons dissolve into stardust and drift out over the Hollow's fields. She watches until they are gone.
  - **If B (`declined`).** She winds the web into the waistcoat's lining and ties Sibil's knot of unbleached silk once more round her lowest wrist. She says seven names, once, aloud.
- **Choices.** One: "Your glass body will never fade. Let the old numbers go." (A) / "You kept your pulse. Carry their names where you go." (B) / "The Vagrant's waiting. Come aboard when you're ready." All play out as the flag says; the third is the exit.
- **Sets.** `catharsis_evolution`.
- **Reward.** None. She boards.
- **If benched.** If Cordelia is not in the party at the epilogue, the beat plays anyway as a short platform scene.
- **Signature line.** "Seven. I counted them all, and that is the whole of the data."
