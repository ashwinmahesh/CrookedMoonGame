## Camp talks

Three personal talks, drafted by Gemini (briefs `Gemini/brief_B_silas.txt`, raw replies `Gemini/reply_B_silas.json`) and converted by Lane F into the camp-file format: the companion's lines as `interject name:silas:`, long lines split, approvals and flags added (the edit log is in `Gemini/README.md`). They are offered at a camp or after a Long Rest, each plays once, and each takes one of the companion's two words a day; they come after any Strained or Doubtful talk and before the Warm, Close and Devoted talks.

- **Unlocks.** Talk 1 `flag.connection_liberation`. Talk 2 `flag.connection_liberation`, the next word after talk 1; it is where `secret_known_silas` is set, at Warm approval or better, which is what lets another hero pay Silas's dark secret at Raum's gate. Talk 3 `flag.liberation_wild_joker`.
- **The talks.**
  1. The tally: a brag over the chalk marks on the inside of his ribs; the turn is the one mark he redraws every night on his breastbone.
  2. The rattle: hearing a lie in the camp the way bones hear wind; the turn is why a good ear is the one stake nobody can take on account.
  3. The deck and the censer: a one-handed flourish; the turn is the herbs that keep his creditor blind through his four hours of revelry.
- **Rules kept.** No roll or check anywhere; every menu has the kind exit "Not now. Rest easy, Silas."; the dark secret is never stated in a camp talk, only approached.
