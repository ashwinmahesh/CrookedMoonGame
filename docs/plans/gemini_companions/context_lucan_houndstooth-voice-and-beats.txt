VOICE SHEET: LUCAN HOUNDSTOOTH.
Male, apparent age 38, a wolf-man monster hunter from Edwardia (a grey province of fog and storm-powered industry) with a spectral hound at his heel. Timbre: deep, velvety, chest-resonant with a soft warm grain, like aged leather and hearth smoke; hushed, close to the microphone, an occasional low involuntary throat-rumble he politely clears. Pace: measured and unhurried, lingering over multi-syllable hunting words; he speeds into flustered over-polite stammers when embarrassed. Accent: a cultured, warm Welsh-inspired lilt, musical, gently rolling on the Rs. Temperament: courteous, solemn and deadpan when calm, treating every horror with scholarly reverence. Under stress a snarl presses up under his vowels and he over-enunciates to sound civilised. He uses old-world hunting words (quarry, spoor, brush, specimen, posture, grade) and says "if you please", "friends" and "good heavens". Pet phrases: "Hush now, the dark has teeth" and "Prime specimen." He must never sound like a gruff Yorkshireman, a brisk West Country rustic, a slobbering werewolf, a camp cartoon villain or a gravelly action grunt.

## The one idea

Lucan walks into the Hollow wearing a stolen legend like an oversized hunting coat, terrified that the beast in his blood is a coward. His thread asks whether an apex predator is made by dominance and show, or by the courage to stand guard without lying about the dark. His spectral hound, Dreadfang, is really the ghost of his childhood lapdog, a Pomeranian called Barnaby, who puffs himself up into a nightmare mastiff so that his master need not walk the woods alone. Everything Barnaby devours makes him larger and fiercer and harder to pass off as a gentleman's sporting companion. The bigger the hound grows, the more he reflects the truth Lucan has buried under grades and polite jargon. Only when Barnaby wears Lucan's own face does the ranger understand that the dog is waiting for him to say how his partner really died.

People invented for this Thread:
- **Owain Pryce**, Lucan's late hunting partner from Baskerton, a cheerful former lamplighter whose silver locket choked the pack beast while Lucan stood frozen in the fog.
- **Lady Morwenna Vance**, chairwoman of the Baskerton Zoological Society, who mounted the beast in her hall and toasted Lucan as its sole slayer. (Gemini wrote "Royal"; cut, because the valley has no crown.)
- **Master Ivor Houndstooth**, Lucan's late grandfather, a Baskerton kennel-master who tucked the runt Pomeranian into young Lucan's coat during a summer storm. (Gemini made Barnaby a terrier; the drafts call him a Pomeranian, so Pomeranian it is.)

## The six beats

### 1. Incitement: the trophy that points

- **Where and when.** The Trophy Room of the Crooked House (H15), the first time Lucan is in the party when the party enters. It plays on entry.
- **Prerequisite.** None.
- **The player sees.** Keziah Endicott's stuffed beasts stand in absurd, theatrical poses round the cold north fireplace, their glass eyes like polished marbles. Lucan sniffs the cedar-and-arsenic air, opens his field journal to grade the taxidermy, and freezes: on a low shelf a small stuffed Pomeranian, no bigger than a boot, has turned its head. Barnaby pads out from behind Lucan's boots, steps into the mount like a hand into a glove, and the little dog points its nose past the attic trapdoor at a stuffed black crow on a high shelf, whose feathers smell faintly of burnt straw. Barnaby yips at a crumb of cheddar on the bear's plinth, and for the first time Lucan does not call it an omen. (No animal is harmed; the mount is only a coat for the ghost.)
- **Choices:**
  - "What's your hound pointing at, Lucan?" Lucan treats the party as colleagues of the hunt and shares his reading of the crow.
  - "Did your omen of death just beg for cheese?" Lucan bristles with flustered dignity, doubling down on the persona.
  - "Leave the mounts. We've a house to clear." Lucan marks the crow in his journal alone, grateful not to be inspected.
- **Sets.** `incitement_slaughter`; `lucan_inc_choice` = `shared` / `bristled` / `alone` (Hilda's greeting reads it). Barnaby grows a handspan at the shoulder; the crow is Lucan's first named quarry.
- **Reward.** Heroic Inspiration to Lucan.
- **If benched.** Pending. The Trophy Room keeps the trigger armed while the house stands; if the house falls first (R22) the beat plays as the echo at the next camp.
- **Signature line.** "Pay no mind to the cheddar, friends. Dreadfang has scented a prime specimen in that crow, and I give the taxidermist four out of ten for the beak."

### 2. Connection: Hilda Brom

- **Where and when.** The road out of Wickermoor, at the village's edge. Hilda "may turn up anywhere", so the trigger is the first departure from the village by any road after the Crooked House is cleansed, with Lucan in the party: she steps out of the brush there. Every Act II journey leaves the village, so it cannot be skipped.
- **Prerequisite.** `incitement_slaughter`.
- **The player sees.** A roll of dry thunder shakes the pines. Barnaby tucks his glowing tail and presses his skull against Lucan's knee, and Lucan covers the hound's ears with a gloved hand. Hilda Brom steps from the brush, a huge spirit bear shimmering faintly behind her shoulders. She eyes the wolf-man shielding his trembling death-omen, and her grim mouth cracks into a sudden booming laugh that startles the crows. She tosses him a sprig of crushed pine needles smeared with crow-grease and points south-east toward Foxwillow's cornfields, where something worse than a fugitive is stalking the rows.
- **Choices:**
  - "You two hunt the same way. Hear her out." Lucan relaxes into courtesy, seeing an equal who knows both the thrill and the terror.
  - "Careful, her spirit bear looks hungry." Lucan straightens his tricorn and matches her stare.
  - "We have our trail. Thanks, Hilda." Lucan tips his hat and pockets the sprig without explaining the thunder.
- **Sets.** `connection_slaughter`; `attitude hilda_brom friendly`; `lucan_hilda_kin` (he sees that she fears the change as he does).
- **Reward.** Hilda is Friendly and Lucan has the Old Ways' keen sight for a day (Bless). The marked pine sprig points the party toward Foxwillow (a marker).
- **If benched.** Hilda still steps out for the party as a hub scene; the Connection is pending, and she turns up again at the next departure.
- **Signature line.** "A gentlewoman of the brush knows that even a harbinger of doom dislikes acoustics in the clouds, Miss Brom, and I accept your trail with my compliments."

### 3. Discovery: the hound grows

- **Where and when.** The Circle of Secrets at the heart of Foxwillow's cornfield (R13), after Raum is destroyed. Lucan in the party.
- **Prerequisite.** `connection_slaughter`.
- **The player sees.** The torn straw and ironbird ribcage of the scarecrow-demon smoulder in the dirt. Barnaby trots past a dropped kernel of kettle corn with only a wistful sniff, lowers his jaws and gulps down the dark, smoking essence rising from Raum's scythe-cut remains. The ghost mastiff swells to the size of a timber wolf, his ribs thickening into heavy spectral bone, his small lapdog ears vanishing into jagged shadow. Overhead a murder of crows wheels across the Crooked Moon, briefly forming a rotting, antlered stag that turns its hollow sockets toward Hartsblight.
- **Choices:**
  - "Your hound is changing. Can you call him back?" Lucan admits unease that little Barnaby is disappearing inside the predator he asked for.
  - "That stag in the crows is our next trophy." Lucan swells with theatrical pride and leans into his hunger.
  - "The field's clear. Back to the road." Lucan whistles Barnaby to heel and quietly records Raum's score.
- **Sets.** `discovery_slaughter`; `lucan_hound_grown` (the Grim's size band rises one step; heavy pawprints leave frost); `augury_hartsblight_stag`. Barnaby no longer begs for scraps.
- **Reward.** One Twist of Fate.
- **If benched.** Pending. Foxwillow stays open; the Circle itself is a demiplane that ends with Raum, so the beat converts to its echo at the next camp (a small scene by the fire where the hound grows).
- **Signature line.** "Hush now, the dark has teeth in those feathers, and by the look of him Dreadfang has acquired a taste."

### 4. Confrontation: the hound's face

- **Where and when.** Gorthos's den (F11) in Hartsblight Forest (R18), after Gorthos is dead. Lucan in the party. (Tobin's beat plays next, at the den's mouth.)
- **Prerequisite.** `discovery_slaughter`.
- **The player sees.** The air reeks of sweet rot and crushed moss round the fallen carcass of the Beast of Blight. Barnaby steps into the bile without hesitation and devours the blighted mist rising from the satyr-monster's antlers until the hound stands as tall as Lucan's chest. On the wet stone walls glowing yellow fungus pulses into a picture: a three-eyed goat crowned with a dragon's fangs, a lion's mane and a serpent's coil. When Barnaby lifts his great head from the remains, the spectral hound is wearing Lucan's own broad-snouted wolf face, and his eyes shine with the exact panicked yellow of the night Owain Pryce died.
- **Choices:**
  - "That hound has your face. What's it showing you?" Lucan lowers his journal; his polite mask slips for the first time.
  - "Stare it down. Show the beast who holds the leash." Lucan bares his teeth in a forced snarl, though his hand trembles at his lapel.
  - "This den is poisoned. Let's get to clean air." Lucan turns from the mural in stiff silence and lets the party lead him out.
- **Sets.** `slaughter_confronted`; `augury_horned_king_goat`.
- **Reward.** +2 to one ability (default Wisdom; the player may redirect; maximum 24).
- **If benched.** Pending. The den stays open until the party leaves the forest; if they have gone, the beat converts to its echo at the next camp.
- **Signature line.** "Keep your lanterns off the wall, if you please, for the fungus has dreadful manners, and my hound is wearing a look I thought I left in Edwardia."

### 5. Climax: the bow

- **Where and when.** The Wytchwood clearing (R24), after the skull ritual is resolved and the Crooked Tree has burned. Lucan in the party.
- **Prerequisite.** `slaughter_confronted`. If Lucan offered his Thread at the skull, this beat does not play.
- **The player sees.** In the smoking ash Barnaby drinks the last steaming black ichor of the Horned King's shadow. Grown to the size of a carriage horse, the spirit beast turns and sinks into a solemn hunter's bow before Lucan, laying a spectral, tooth-marked silver locket at his boots, while the quiet silhouette of Owain Pryce stands in the smoke behind it. Lucan takes off his tricorn; his Welsh voice has no swagger left. He tells the party how he froze in the Baskerton fog while a pack beast mauled Owain, how the beast choked to death on Owain's locket, and how he let Lady Morwenna Vance toast him as the hero. At the plain truth the monstrous hound's harsh glare softens into Barnaby's gentle eyes, waiting to see what they become. Owain's ghost lifts a hand in quiet forgiveness and fades into the embers. (The party witnesses; nobody prompts him.)
- **Choices** (the book's A and B, personalised):
  - **A**, the bigger price: "Take his paw. Be the guardian this wild needs." Lucan merges with Barnaby in peaceful acceptance, becomes the apex spirit of the dark woods, and stays in the valley when the train departs.
  - **B**: "Keep your coat and your name. Hunt with us." He declines the merge and Barnaby shrinks back into a loyal lapdog ghost, to hunt honest quarry in the waking world.
  - Quiet way on: "It's your hunt, Lucan. Step forward when ready." Lucan makes his peace on his own terms, and the choice resolves as B.
- **Sets.** `climax_slaughter`; `thread_slaughter_catharsis` = `taken` (A) or `declined` (B); `lucan_confessed`.
- **Reward.** A feat: **Savage Attacker** (alternate Sharpshooter).
- **If benched.** The clearing stays open after the burn as the epilogue's "Wytchwood ash" place; the Last Ride prompt names his unfinished hunt. If the player boards anyway, the quiet Catharsis plays.
- **If he offered his Thread at the skull.** No Climax. The hound stays at the mastiff's size at his heel, and the field journal gets no new grade.
- **If he gave his life at the skull (the Last Act).** The slide shows a small ghost dog lying on a hunting coat in the Wytchwood's ash, and a tricorn hat on a stump.
- **Signature line.** "Owain killed the beast with the locket round his throat while I shook in the gorse, and I will not take your bow under a stolen name, Barnaby."

### 6. Catharsis: the good boy

- **Where and when.** The village and the platform (R25), in the epilogue's last scenes before the Last Ride. Plays by the Climax flag.

Hilda Brom watches from the inn's porch and raises two fingers in a silent hunter's salute. In the mist, a very large ghost dog is heard barking once, politely, at a clap of thunder, and then not afraid of it.

- **Reward.** None.

## How his arc meets the others

- **Lucan and Rook** (the mirrored pair: both froze, both took credit). Two scenes. Early and light, in the Founder's Round in the village (R11): Barnaby sits squarely on Rook's armoured boot and gives a solemn yip at the grave knight's pocket; Rook deadpans that an unlicensed phantom mastiff violates three municipal leash statutes, and Lucan insists the hound has sensed a doom-laden draught. Later and plain, at the ruins of the Crooked House (R22), after Rook's second monument, when the secrets are known: the mirror scene in Rook's file, where Rook tears up the leash-law citation and lets the dog gnaw the brass.
- **Lucan and Tobin.** Near the edge of the Foxwillow cornfield (R13), Lucan pauses beside Tobin and takes a long, appreciative sniff of his straw and woodsmoke, remarks in his hushed commentator's baritone that Tobin carries the finest autumn bouquet in the hollow, and scores it nine out of ten for rustic comfort. Barnaby drops a stick at Tobin's boots: the only party member whose hem he never chews.
