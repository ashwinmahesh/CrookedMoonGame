## Camp talks

Three personal talks, drafted by Gemini (briefs `Gemini/brief_B_tobin.txt`, raw replies `Gemini/reply_B_tobin.json`) and converted by Lane F into the camp-file format: the companion's lines as `interject name:tobin:`, long lines split, approvals and flags added (the edit log is in `Gemini/README.md`). They are offered at a camp or after a Long Rest, each plays once, and each takes one of the companion's two words a day; they come after any Strained or Doubtful talk and before the Warm, Close and Devoted talks.

- **Unlocks.** Talk 1 `flag.connection_rejuvenation`. Talk 2 `flag.connection_rejuvenation`, the next word after talk 1; it is where `secret_known_tobin` is set, at Warm approval or better, which is what lets another hero pay Tobin's dark secret at Raum's gate. Talk 3 `flag.rejuvenation_confronted`.
- **The talks.**
  1. The head: shaking out beetles three paces from the fire; the turn is that he cannot sleep with woodsmoke on his burlap.
  2. Frayed seams: why Morgan and Randall may not mend him; the slip is the wedding flowers in his chest.
  3. Poppets: a tiny wicker shield on every poppet; the turn is that he has never woven one for himself.
- **Rules kept.** No roll or check anywhere; every menu has the kind exit "Not now. Rest easy, Tobin."; the dark secret is never stated in a camp talk, only approached.

## The Connection scene (dialogue draft)

Sister Rain's meeting with Tobin, drafted the same way and converted the same way. It is a few nodes in Sister Rain's own conversation file, started by the trigger in beat 2. Bless for a day, the ally and the Heroic Inspiration are rewards the Fateweaving statements will give (listed in the overview); the `set` and `attitude` lines are the ones the grammar has today.

```text
# Tobin meets Sister Rain (a node of Sister Rain's own conversation file; the trigger is in the beat above).
# Bless for a day and Heroic Inspiration need the Fateweaving statements the overview lists; they are not written here.

~ rain_tobin_start
sister_rain [smile]: That is a surgeon's knot on a pumpkin stem, friend, and the sap is already settling.
sister_rain [sad]: Oh, your poor collarbone is as dry and aching as the soil under this green.
sister_rain [neutral]: The Patient Lady says we tend what grows and bind what splits, and we never answer blight with a torch.
interject name:tobin: Careful on the collarbone, miss, it's old wood and dry.
interject name:tobin: A clean splice stops the bleeding, that is just sound husbandry, no sermon required.
* Her way makes sense. You can't heal soil by scorching it. -> rain_gentle
* Patience is fine for daisies, not for a plague. -> rain_blunt
* Take her blessing. We should keep moving. -> rain_go

~ rain_gentle
sister_rain [smile]: You hear that, mister scarecrow, your friends have the right of it.
sister_rain [neutral]: Ash never grew an apple yet, no matter how deep the rot went.
interject name:tobin: Good soil, bad seed, and a great deal of weeding left to do before anyone talks of apples.
-> rain_close

~ rain_blunt
sister_rain [angry]: Bite your tongue, the land is bleeding enough without you calling down iron and fire on it!
sister_rain [smile]: Listen to me roaring like a March gale when your friend here just saved my best pumpkin.
interject name:tobin: You have a brisk way with a congregation, I will give you that much.
-> rain_close

~ rain_go
sister_rain [smile]: Always rushing off, you travellers, though at least this one stops to mend a vine.
interject name:tobin: A split stem is a split stem, and it takes ten seconds to tie it right.
-> rain_close

~ rain_close
sister_rain [smile]: Walk in the Lady's patience, stitched man, and don't pluck what grows from your cuff.
sister_rain [sad]: When you have the stomach for worse than a bruised vine, the sick places round the village are crying out for a steady hand.
set connection_rejuvenation
attitude sister_rain friendly
-> END
```
