VOICE SHEET: DELPHINE RAVELLE.
Female, apparent age 26, a living patchwork doll from Picco (a circus province) and a warlock whose patron is a floating jester's head she calls "Your Foolishness". Timbre: bright, brassy and velvety, with a dry snap on consonants like briskly shuffled cards; a warm cabaret chest register; when she drops the showmanship her voice thins to a quiet, papery hush. Pace: brisk and bouncy, rattling through alliterative setups like a carnival barker, then a sharp half-beat pause before the barb. Accent: an old-fashioned American vaudeville stage voice, forward and projected. Temperament: sparkling, imperious, irrepressibly amused when calm. Under stress the music flattens out and she turns rapid, clipped and brittle. She uses stage and tailoring words (on the bias, cheap seats, headliner, seams, hem, billing, house, curtain). Pet phrases: "Mind the seams, darling" and "He says you're lying, and he doesn't even have eyes." She calls people "darling" and "darlings". She must never sound like a squeaky toy, a robot, a manic giggling harlequin or a modern Valley girl.

## The one idea

Her Thread is about the cost of top billing, and whether a self-made soul can be stitched out of someone else's ruin. Delphine believes her floating patron, the Great Fool, is an ancient power promising her a throne where nobody can unpick her seams. In truth the Fool is what remains of her mortal rival from Funtopia, a gentle clown whose warning bells she cut so she could take his headline act. Every beat returns to one image: a bell that never rang, the applause that followed a scream, and the crown waiting in the wings. Her choice at the end is not between good and evil, but between the lonely immortality she killed for and surrendering the spotlight to the partner she betrayed.

People invented for this Thread:
- **Ambrose Finch**, billed as "Bells-and-All" Ambrose, the warm-hearted high-wire clown of her Picco troupe, whose rigging bells she snipped before the midnight show; now the floating head over her shoulder. (Gemini named him Barnaby; renamed because Lucan's ghost dog is Barnaby.)
- **Master Orsino**, the crabbed toymaker of Funtopia who stitched her from leftover carnival felt and told her she was only stuffing meant to take a fall. (Gemini named him Ambrogio; renamed so he does not sound like Ambrose.)
- **Lottie Brass**, ringmistress of the Midnight Menagerie, who handed Delphine the headline contract while the crowd was still cheering in the dark.

## The six beats

### 1. Incitement: the handbill

- **Where and when.** The Harlequin's car (G7) on the Ghostlight Express (R10), the first time Delphine is in the party when the party enters it. The ghost of a jester in a belled cap hangs from a trapeze above, performing painful pratfalls.
- **Prerequisite.** None.
- **The player sees.** The shamed jester ghost crashes through his routine overhead while Delphine peels a damp handbill for the Festival of Fools off the mahogany panelling; the ink smells of vinegar and sour paste. Over her left shoulder the Great Fool stops spinning, stares at the painted ring on the bill, and hums a tinny three-note circus fanfare without making a single joke. Her fingers twitch toward her wrist thread, and she slips her ink-stained notebook from her lining to copy the border of crowns and bells.
- **Choices:**
  - "Read us the fine print. Is that your crowd?" Delphine treats the party as co-conspirators in her grand debut.
  - "Your floating friend knows that stage already." Delphine bristles and wonders whether the Fool leads her to a throne or a trap.
  - "Leave the paper. We need the front cars." Delphine pockets the poster and resolves to decode the omen alone.
- **Sets.** `incitement_ascendancy`; `delphine_inc_choice` = `shared` / `bristled` / `alone` (Alda's greeting reads it). She claims the poster; the Fool's silence plants a first prickle of doubt.
- **Reward.** Heroic Inspiration to Delphine.
- **If benched.** The Harlequin's car is re-enterable until the crash; after that, pending, with the echo at the next camp.
- **Signature line.** "Look at that billing, darlings, a whole festival of fools and not a single headliner worth the paste on the wall."

### 2. Connection: Alda Farnum

- **Where and when.** The tavern of the Green Queen Inn (V1), near its small theatre stage. The party must sleep at the inn, so the trigger is the first time Delphine is in the party when the party enters the tavern after the Crooked House is cleansed.
- **Prerequisite.** `incitement_ascendancy`.
- **The player sees.** Alda Farnum pauses with a tray of spiced wine, her eyes fixed on the empty air above Delphine's left shoulder. She smells of pressed lavender and cold copper. She reaches, not toward Delphine, but to smooth the invisible lapel of the floating Fool, and remarks in her honeyed voice that such patrons rarely linger without a debt on the ledger. She offers a house blessing in exchange for the story of how a ragdoll from Picco came by him. Delphine's glass button eye gleams: two women who both want to be queens take each other's measure.
- **Choices:**
  - "Let her read the ledger. A free blessing's good business." Delphine trades theatrical half-truths and feels validated in her hunger for a crown.
  - "Careful, she's pricing your stitches like cloth." Delphine sharpens her tongue against bargains that sound like ownership.
  - "We want a room, not a reading." Delphine cuts it short with a brittle curtsy and lets the blessing settle over her like cold powder.
- **Sets.** `connection_ascendancy`; `attitude alda_farnum friendly`; Alda confirms that the Fool is tied to Delphine by an old, personal tether, not a whim (`delphine_tether_noted`).
- **Reward.** Alda is Friendly and Delphine is blessed for a day (Bless).
- **If benched.** The tavern is open every night; Alda greets the party without it, and the Connection is pending with a marker on V1 (Alda glances at the empty air, which should be a first-sight tell for the later reveal).
- **Signature line.** "She has a lovely smile for a woman counting my buttons, and Your Foolishness says her ledger is written in somebody else's red ink."

### 3. Discovery: the belfry

- **Where and when.** The Crimson Monastery (R14), M14 the Watchful Belfry: a wooden platform under a dome on four pillars, red and orange braziers, a great silver bell with its rope tied off, bats in the rafters, a view over the lake's mist to the village lights. The first time the party climbs to the belfry with Delphine, reachable without any invitation. If Father Renathyr is there (the Witching Hour), the beat holds until his scene ends; it plays in a quiet beat after he leaves.
- **Prerequisite.** `connection_ascendancy`.
- **The player sees.** Delphine stops dead before the great silver bell. She reaches into her coat pocket and touches a cold, clapperless brass rigging bell she has carried since Funtopia. Before she can speak the Great Fool plunges headfirst into an orange hellfire brazier, drinks the flame and rises with a translucent smoke-woven torso draped in scorched motley. Looking at the bound bell he recites a rhyme about a king who cut the clapper so the fool would take the fall, and how a silent bell still counts the hours until the crown comes due.
- **Choices:**
  - "Write that rhyme down before the smoke clears." Delphine scribbles furiously in her notebook and treats the accusation as a royal puzzle.
  - "He was looking at that rope. Whose bell?" Delphine goes rigid and clipped and admits the rise in Picco cost another performer.
  - "The monastery's waking. Step away." Delphine shoves the notebook back in her lining with shaking fingers.
- **Sets.** `discovery_ascendancy`; `delphine_fool_body_smoke` (the Fool now has a smoky torso); the notebook gains its first verse she does not need to guess at.
- **Reward.** One Twist of Fate.
- **If benched.** Pending. The belfry stays open while the monastery stands; after Renathyr falls it remains reachable (the monastery is a place), and the beat re-arms there. The Narrator says once as the party leaves the belfry without her: "The bell hangs silent. It has been waiting for somebody."
- **Signature line.** "A bell with its throat tied is just a bucket of silver, and if he keeps rhyming on the bias, I will unpick his jaw myself."

### 4. Confrontation: the Fool's new body

- **Where and when.** Fool's Day (R21), the Ring of Fools, after Chuckles dies. A wooden puppet's shattered remains lie in the dirt. Delphine in the party.
- **Prerequisite.** `discovery_ascendancy`.
- **The player sees.** The crowd's manic laughter dies into the smell of trampled mud and burnt sugar. The Great Fool drifts down from her shoulder, gathers the splintered limbs into his smoky chest, and knits them into a tall, seamless body of polished joint-wood and porcelain that needs no thread at all: whole, graceful, impossible to unpick, the exact immortal perfection Delphine has starved for. He sweeps her a low, silent bow, and for the first time speaks clearly: the herald is dressed, the crooked goat waits in the ash, and the monarch who takes the throne must sit alone for ever.
- **Choices:**
  - "Look at him. You can become real too." Delphine stares hungrily at his seamless joints, convinced the crown is worth any isolation.
  - "You understood that. He's warning you off." Delphine drops her barker's cadence and admits the rhyme terrified her because it sounded like a real voice.
  - "The ring's clearing. Collect yourself." Delphine turns her back on the newly whole Fool and clutches her frayed cuff so no one sees it tremble.
- **Sets.** `ascendancy_confronted`, `ascendancy_fool_reborn`.
- **Reward.** +2 to one ability (default Charisma; the player may redirect; maximum 24).
- **If benched.** Pending. The festival state ends when Chuckles dies and the ring goes with it, so the beat converts at once to its echo at the next camp. The last-door reminder (before the party strikes Chuckles) names any companion with an unplayed beat.
- **Signature line.** "Not a single stitch showing on him, not a seam to pull, and for once in his afterlife I understood every rotten word."

### 5. Climax: the crown

- **Where and when.** The Wytchwood clearing (R24), after the skull ritual is resolved and the Crooked Tree has burned. Delphine in the party.
- **Prerequisite.** `ascendancy_confronted`. If Delphine offered her Thread at the skull, this beat does not play.
- **The player sees.** Hot white ash settles over the Wytchwood; the Crooked Queen hangs bound in the moon. The Great Fool, in his seamless body, steps into the cinders and lifts a crown woven of grey ash and curled goat horn, its rim hung with tiny brass bells whose clappers have been snipped away. For one heartbeat his jester's grin softens into the lopsided bow of Ambrose Finch on the high wire, offering her the headline a second time without a word of reproach. Delphine stands between the party she fought beside and the immortal, seamless sovereignty she killed to reach.
- **Choices** (the book's A and B, personalised):
  - **A**, the bigger price: "Take the crown. You paid for the headline." She is crowned an otherworldly monarch whose felt and thread harden to untouchable porcelain, bound to rule a solitary stage. She leaves the party at the Last Ride.
  - **B**: "Give it to him. Ambrose earned the top of the bill." She sets the crown on the Fool's head and stays a patchwork woman among her friends, mortal, frayed and free.
  - Quiet way on: "Step back from the ash. Settle the bill yourselves." Delphine decides alone and the choice resolves as B.
- **Sets.** `climax_ascendancy`; `thread_ascendancy_catharsis` = `taken` (A) or `declined` (B); `delphine_named_ambrose` (she says his name here, for the first time).
- **Reward.** A feat: **Fey-Touched** (alternate Musician).
- **If benched.** The clearing stays open after the burn as the epilogue's "Wytchwood ash" place; the Last Ride prompt names her unfinished crown. If the player boards anyway, the quiet Catharsis plays.
- **If she offered her Thread at the skull.** No Climax; at the epilogue the Fool floats on her shoulder, silent, and she does not snap at him.
- **If she gave her life at the skull (the Last Act).** The slide shows a crown of ash on a stump, and one working brass bell hanging from a cuff-thread on a branch.
- **Signature line.** "Look at the bells on the rim, not a sound in the lot of them, and he still holds the spotlight open for me."

### 6. Catharsis: the bell

- **Where and when.** The platform in the fog (R25), as the Ghostlight Express arrives, in the epilogue's last scene. Plays by the Climax flag.
- **The player sees.** Delphine stands under the station lamp.
  - **If A (`taken`).** Her coat is stiff with silver frost and her button eye is cold starlight. She tosses her ink-stained notebook into a brazier, because a queen needs no further guesses, gives the party one last sweeping ringmaster's bow, and walks off into the mist alone with her chin high. She does not board.
  - **If B (`declined`).** The newly crowned Fool tips his horned coronet to her from the platform's edge, reaches out one porcelain hand and ties a single working brass bell onto her frayed cuff, and vanishes into the fog as the whistle blows. Delphine pulls a length of red thread from her wrist and stitches the bell fast. She boards with the party, ringing it once with her thumb.
- **Choices.** One: "A curtain call. Don't let them see you look back." (A) / "That bell suits you better than a crown." (B) / "The Vagrant's waiting." All play out as the flag says; the third is the exit.
- **Sets.** `catharsis_ascendancy`.
- **Reward.** None.
- **If benched.** If Delphine is not in the party at the epilogue, this beat plays anyway as a short platform scene.
- **Signature line.** "The show is over, darlings, and whether you loved the act or hated the turn, nobody can say I didn't write the ending."

- **The confession, if Delphine pays it herself.** "Ambrose always checked his high-wire rig by ear, because the dark under the big top blinds you. I took my shears up the ladder before the midnight show and snipped every brass clapper off his warning line. There was a snap in the rafters, a long roar from the midnight crowd, and then nothing where he used to be, so I walked out and took his bow."
- **The Narrator.** "The gate drinks the silence between the snapped wire and the applause, and swings wide for a headliner who climbed over an empty rope."
- **The proof.** A handful of tarnished brass rigging bells with the clappers sheared off inside, wrapped in a scrap of faded Picco marquee velvet.
- **If Delphine pays it** (`paid_own_secret_delphine`): Raum gains a hold on Delphine. The Great Fool, over her shoulder, does not make a pun for the rest of the chapter. Her approval of the player rises by 2.
- **If another hero pays it** (possible only when `secret_known_delphine`, set by camp talk 2 at Warm approval or better): rumour in Foxwillow and Wickermoor (`rumour_delphine_bells`), her approval falls by 8, and the first camp talk afterwards is the cold one.
