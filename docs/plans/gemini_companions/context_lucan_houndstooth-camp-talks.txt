## Camp talks

Three personal talks, drafted by Gemini (briefs `Gemini/brief_B_lucan.txt`, raw replies `Gemini/reply_B_lucan.json`) and converted by Lane F into the camp-file format: the companion's lines as `interject name:lucan:`, long lines split, approvals and flags added (the edit log is in `Gemini/README.md`). They are offered at a camp or after a Long Rest, each plays once, and each takes one of the companion's two words a day; they come after any Strained or Doubtful talk and before the Warm, Close and Devoted talks.

- **Unlocks.** Talk 1 `flag.connection_slaughter`. Talk 2 `flag.connection_slaughter`, the next word after talk 1; it is where `secret_known_lucan` is set, at Warm approval or better, which is what lets another hero pay Lucan's dark secret at Raum's gate. Talk 3 `flag.slaughter_confronted`.
- **The talks.**
  1. The grades: his field journal's marks out of ten; the turn is that numbers steady his hands.
  2. Dreadfang: the grand lineage that falls apart with the thunder and a crust of cheese.
  3. Edwardia: Baskerton's chimneys and fog; the turn is Owain's laugh in the present tense.
- **Rules kept.** No roll or check anywhere; every menu has the kind exit "Not now. Rest easy, Lucan."; the dark secret is never stated in a camp talk, only approached.
