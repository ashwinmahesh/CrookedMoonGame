It is set in Wickermoor Hollow, a haunted valley in Druskenvald, a land of endless night under a giant Crooked Moon with a crone's face. The look is a Halloween that never ends, drawn like an 1980s cartoon. A ghost train, the Ghostlight Express, has crashed in the valley; the party is stranded in the Hollow and its village, Wickermoor. The tone is warm, wry and funny on the surface, with real dread and real grief underneath. The player controls a party of four chosen from six companions: Rook Coldwake, Tobin Hayward, Silas Vane, Delphine Ravelle, Lucan Houndstooth and Cordelia Skein. They are quirky, capable adults.

He speaks to the party as "you" and about the companion in the third person, by first name. Present tense, one or two sentences, under about 32 words.
