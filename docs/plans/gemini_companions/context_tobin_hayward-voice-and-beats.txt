VOICE SHEET: TOBIN HAYWARD.
Male, apparent age 31, a scarecrow druid (straw and burlap) who is a pragmatic rural diagnostician of curses and rot. Timbre: dry, fibrous, tactile, with a warm papery rustle and a clear woody core, close-mic'd and intimate, a subtle reediness on long vowels. Pace: brisk and purposeful, clipping through diagnoses and observations, then stopping on a dime for a dry two-beat pause. Accent: West Country British pastoral, rounded rhotic Rs, warm open vowels, a rhythmic country lilt that stays crisp. Temperament: unsentimental, fussy, briskly diagnostic when calm, like a country surgeon inspecting a blighted orchard. Under stress, especially near open flame, his breath shortens and his words crowd together. He uses farming and weaving words (soil, rows, ledger, canker, taproot, seam, fraying, stitch). Pet phrases: "Good soil, bad seed" and "Hold still, you're fraying." He must never sound like a slow country bumpkin, a booming forest prophet, a creaky Halloween monster or a dreamy hippie herbalist. He is not a motherly caretaker; he is a crisp field surgeon who is a control freak about everyone's seams.

## The one idea

When a field is choked with blight, where is the line between tending the living root and burning the whole acre to ash? In his mortal life, in a famine, Tobin chose the torch, and he woke in Druskenvald as dry straw that panics at a single spark. His brisk, clinical habits (scraping bark, tying off cuffs) are how he avoids looking at the charred ledger of his past. Each sick place in the valley asks him to diagnose someone else's answer to grief and rot: Petunia's bergamot, Skitterdeep's plague, Farryn's poisoned spring. Only when the Crooked Tree falls must he hold live fire against his own ribs and decide whether flame can cleanse without murdering what was loved.

People invented for this Thread:
- **Hollis Garrow**, his mortal neighbour in the next orchard, a whistling widower who carved wooden field-mice for the village children. (Gemini named him Barnaby Holt; renamed because Lucan's ghost dog is Barnaby.)
- **Tamsin Hayward**, Tobin's mortal wife, a flax-weaver who braided meadow-rue and white clover into his coat on their wedding morning, taken by the famine year. Her flowers are the dried bundle in his chest.

## The six beats

### 1. Incitement: the bergamot tree

- **Where and when.** The Conservatory of the Crooked House (R12), the first time Tobin is in the party when the party steps in. It plays on entry.
- **Prerequisite.** None.
- **The player sees.** Rain hammers the cracked glass roof. Petunia Lockwood's ghost stares through the one living tree, the bergamot with its green fruit. Tobin steps past the dead vines, presses a burlap thumb to the tree's weeping bark, and jerks his hand back as if the sap scalded him. In the tree's vision black rot splits the trunk and runs out across the valley toward a hill crawling with pale centipedes, and a phantom smell of scorching apple-wood sets the mice in his lapels squeaking and diving for his pockets. He pulls three straws from his ribs, twists a stiff splint round a cracked bergamot twig, and clears his throat hard to cover the shake in his fingers.
- **Choices:**
  - "What did that tree just show you?" Tobin steadies and commits to tracking the rot to its source.
  - "Your hand shook. Did the sap burn you?" Tobin feels seen past the mask and admits the rot reminds him of an old orchard fire.
  - "Leave the tree. We have a manor to clear." Tobin pockets his pruning knife and keeps the diagnosis to himself.
- **Sets.** `incitement_rejuvenation`; `tobin_inc_choice` = `asked` / `admitted` / `moved_on` (Sister Rain's greeting reads it). He marks Skitterdeep on his mental map.
- **Reward.** Heroic Inspiration to Tobin.
- **If benched.** Pending (`pending_rejuvenation_incitement`). The Conservatory keeps the trigger armed while the estate stands; if the house falls first (R22) the beat plays as the echo at the next camp.
- **Signature line.** "Bark's cold, nothing burning here, only a deep sour canker running down the taproot toward that centipede hill."

### 2. Connection: Sister Rain

- **Where and when.** The Pavilion, the small gazebo on the green beside the road that winds from the estate gate down into the village. Because the road passes it, the trigger is walking down from the estate for the first time after the house is cleansed, with Tobin in the party. The beat cannot be walked past.
- **Prerequisite.** `incitement_rejuvenation`.
- **The player sees.** Sister Rain kneels in the mud tying a bruised pumpkin vine to a stake, her silver sunflower pendant swinging against her green dress. Tobin squats beside her, pulls a twist of straw from his cuff and binds the stem above the split with a neat rural knot that stops the sap bleeding. Her eyes go from storm-cloud to bright morning as she presses two fingers to his wooden collarbone, feeling the dry ache in his frame beside the valley's sickness. She speaks of the Patient Lady's way: tending what grows and never answering blight with a torch. Tobin goes very still, then nods once with his hat pulled low. (She does not know his secret and does not preach at him.)
- **Choices:**
  - "Her way makes sense. You can't heal soil by scorching it." Tobin accepts the rebuke to his old methods.
  - "Patience is fine for daisies, not for a plague." Tobin hardens, grateful for the map though not for the mercy.
  - "Take her blessing. We should keep moving." Tobin tips his hat and folds her warning away.
- **Sets.** `connection_rejuvenation`; `attitude sister_rain friendly`.
- **Reward.** Sister Rain is Friendly and Tobin has a day's clear sight of the land's veins (Bless). A pale primrose sprouts from the stitching of his left cuff and stays there. She marks the deeper festering places round the village.
- **If benched.** The trigger still fires for the party (she is on the road), but without Tobin it is only a hub scene; the Connection stays pending with a marker on the Pavilion, and she is there each Twilight until he meets her.
- **Signature line.** "She knows her grafting, I'll give her that, even if she thinks a gentle rain washes out a fifty-year rot."

### 3. Discovery: the birch at the winch tower

- **Where and when.** Skitterdeep Mine (R16), outside the winch tower, after Stonoga Blackstinger is dead and the plague has lifted. Tobin in the party and Stonoga's notes read.
- **Prerequisite.** `connection_rejuvenation`.
- **The player sees.** Stonoga's notes are tucked into Tobin's duster. A stunted birch by the lift shakes off its grey centipede webbing. Tobin pinches a flake of its peeling bark onto his burlap tongue to read the vintage, and stiffens as the living wood answers his touch: a vision of Hartsblight Forest smothered in weeping shelf-fungus round the ribs of a rotting deer. He spits the bark into his palm, wipes his stitched mouth with his sleeve, and checks that the mice in his coat are breathing clean air.
- **Choices:**
  - "The mine's clean. Take a breath first." Tobin allows himself a moment of pride in a balanced row.
  - "Her notes and that vision point to Hartsblight." Tobin throws himself into the work with sharpened urgency.
  - "Keep the vision to yourself. We're moving." Tobin knots a wicker token on a branch and marches ahead.
- **Sets.** `discovery_rejuvenation`; `augury_hartsblight_rot`.
- **Reward.** One Twist of Fate.
- **If benched.** Pending. The mine stays open; the birch is still at the lift and the Narrator says once, leaving without him, "A young birch by the lift shivers, as if it had been about to say something." If the plague clock (village phase) has not yet ended when Tobin joins, the beat still waits for Stonoga.
- **Signature line.** "One parish cleared of crawlers, and already the timber up the ridge is coughing up stag-rot and wet mushroom."

### 4. Confrontation: the cleansed trees

- **Where and when.** Hartsblight Forest (R18), at the mouth of Gorthos's den (F11), after Gorthos is dead and the blight is gone. Tobin in the party.
- **Prerequisite.** `discovery_rejuvenation`.
- **The player sees.** The spores crumble to harmless grey dust. Tobin touches the cleansed trunk of an ancient ash at the den's mouth and sees the Green Queen twisting into the Crooked Tree at the heart of the valley's curse. He presses a hand flat on his chest, where the dried wedding flowers are braided into his core straw, and he thinks of Farryn, who loved something enough to let the world rot for it. He weaves a small, clean wicker stag and sets it at the den's mouth for Farryn, and straightens up with his turnip-lantern swaying cold against his collarbone.
- **Choices:**
  - "Farryn loved enough to rot the world. You know that ache." Tobin owns the grief in his chest and swears not to let love make him a destroyer twice.
  - "Grief doesn't excuse poisoning the well." Tobin draws a surgeon's line between mourning the dead and spoiling the living ground.
  - "The spring is clear. Let's go." Tobin leaves the stag on the stone and turns his back.
- **Sets.** `rejuvenation_confronted`; `augury_crooked_tree_root`.
- **Reward.** +2 to one ability (default Wisdom; the player may redirect; maximum 24).
- **If benched.** Pending. The den's mouth stays open; if the party has passed on to the cemetery (R19) the Rejuvenation marker waits on the forest map, and the echo plays at the next camp if the player does not go back.
- **Signature line.** "You keep the pressed flower in your ribs, girl. You don't tip the whole parish in the cesspit to fetch the spring back."

### 5. Climax: the embers

- **Where and when.** The Wytchwood clearing (R24), after the skull ritual is resolved and the Crooked Tree has burned. Tobin in the party.
- **Prerequisite.** `rejuvenation_confronted`. If Tobin offered his Thread at the skull, this beat does not play.
- **The player sees.** The Crooked Tree is a bed of white-hot coals; the Crooked Queen is bound in the crone-faced moon. The old trees show Tobin a vision of the whole wood catching flame to birth an uncorrupted age. A live golden ember rolls to the toe of his boot, and the dry straw in his wrists begins to smoke as his breath shortens. He unbraids the dried bundle of Tamsin's wedding meadow-rue and white clover from his chest, the only living memory he kept, and holds it between the ember and the waiting roots.
- **Choices:**
  - **A**, the bigger price: "Light the flowers, Tobin." He burns his wedding bundle in his own scorched hands, carries the fire to the grove's four corners, and the Wytchwood burns and blooms. Then the player picks the season (below).
  - **B**: "Stamp it out. No more orchards burned." He crushes the coal under his heel, tucks the scorched flowers back in his ribs, and sees blighted farmland beyond the valley.
  - Quiet way on: "The Queen is bound. Step back from the coals." He steps three paces from the heat, keeps the flowers safe, and sees the sick lands on the horizon as the party leaves. (Resolves as B.)
  - **If A, the season** (sets `tobin_season`): "Spring first." (snowdrops and green apple-buds through the char) / "A full summer." (clover and bees) / "A clean autumn." (cider apples, copper oak leaves) / "Winter, to rest the soil." (hoarfrost, silver pine needles).
- **Sets.** `climax_rejuvenation`; `thread_rejuvenation_catharsis` = `taken` (A) or `declined` (B); `tobin_season`; `tobin_named_hollis` (he says the neighbour's name only here, to the fire).
- **Reward.** A feat: **Healer** (alternate Resilient, Wisdom).
- **If benched.** The clearing stays open after the burn as the epilogue's "Wytchwood ash" place (short walk, no fights); the Last Ride prompt names Tobin's unfinished fire. If the player boards anyway, the quiet Catharsis plays.
- **If he offered his Thread at the skull.** No Climax. The wedding bundle stays in his chest, and the epilogue shows him keeping three paces from every lantern on the platform.
- **If he gave his life at the skull (the Last Act).** The slide shows a single green shoot in the ash of the Wytchwood, with a primrose at its foot.
- **Signature line.** "I'm sorry, Hollis. I barred your door in the cinder-smoke, so let this spark take my own chest-straw first."

### 6. Catharsis: the firebox

- **Where and when.** The station in the fog at the village's edge (R25), as the Ghostlight Express arrives, in the epilogue's last scene before the Last Ride. Plays by the Climax flag.
- **The player sees.** Mist curls round the rails; the Vagrant leans from the cab. Tobin sits on a crate with his stitched head resting on his knees, shaking a pinch of Wytchwood ash from his hatband, his mice grooming on his shoulder.
  - **If A (`taken`).** A small shoot of the chosen season grows straight out of the scorched burlap of his right palm. He stands beside the firebox and does not step back.
  - **If B (`declined`).** He waxes a bundle of fresh pruning knives tied with a stem of dry meadow-rue, keeping his three paces and, for the first time, not apologising for them.
- **Choices.** One: "The Vagrant's waiting, Tobin." / "Your ledger's balanced. Come aboard." / "Ready for the next parish?" All lead to the footplate.
- **Sets.** `catharsis_rejuvenation`.
- **Reward.** None.
- **If benched.** If Tobin is not in the party at the epilogue, this beat plays anyway as a short scene at the platform; benching never takes the Catharsis.
- **Signature line.** "No debt left in the dirt here. The rows are straight, and the firebox doesn't frighten me half so much as it did."

## The dark secret (Raum's gate, R13)

- **The confession, if Tobin pays it himself.** "In the dry famine year in Enoch the black canker took the orchard next to mine. My neighbour was in his barn trying to save his seed-apples, and when he would not let me torch his trees, I dropped the bar across his door and set the thatch alight myself. The wind stayed west, my rows lived through the autumn, and I listened to his roof fall in before I went home to supper."
- **The Narrator.** "The gate drinks the apple-smoke out of his burlap. The hinges come open slick with soot."
- **The proof.** A heat-warped iron barn latch fused to a lump of melted green bottle-glass, and a carved wooden mouse charred half away, at the bottom of his herb satchel.
- **If Tobin pays it** (`paid_own_secret_tobin`): Raum gains a hold on Tobin. Tobin's approval of the player falls by 1 and rises by 3 at the next camp (he has said it aloud and did not break). The others hear it; Sister Rain does not.
- **If another hero pays it** (possible only when `secret_known_tobin`, set by camp talk 2 at Warm approval or better): rumour in Foxwillow and Wickermoor (`rumour_tobin_orchard`), Tobin's approval falls by 8, and the first camp talk afterwards is the cold one.
- **Reveal reactions.** Silas, who rigged a draw to give up his own brother, says nothing; the next night he leaves his flint-lighter at the bottom of his pack and does not mention it.

## How his arc meets the others

- **Tobin and Silas** (the friction pair, turning to friends). Optional; plays only if both are in the party. At Foxwillow's burning windmill (R13), Tobin freezes three paces from the roaring timber until Silas snaps his brass flint-lighter shut, tucks it in his belt and hauls a bucket of ditch-water past him. Later, during the Skitterdeep plague in the village (R16), Tobin uncorks one of Silas's supposedly fake tonics, tastes the costly fever-bark brewed into it and quietly weaves a wicker bone-charm into the lining of Silas's cloak. Neither calls it charity.
- **Tobin and Cordelia.** At the stones in Hartsblight Forest (R18), Cordelia crouches beside him as he drains spoiled sap from a blighted root into her glass vials. He shows her how to braid three dry rushes to filter the spores, and she trades him a strip of waxed linen for his frayed wrist seams. She is allowed to catalogue the vintage of every curse he scrapes off his boot.
- **Tobin and Rook.** Rook plants his shield between Tobin and a smouldering fence post at the first monument in Foxwillow (see Rook's file).
