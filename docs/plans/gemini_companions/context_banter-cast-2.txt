THE SIX COMPANIONS, part 2 of 2.

VOICE SHEET: DELPHINE RAVELLE (speaker id: delphine_ravelle).
Female, apparent age 26, a living patchwork doll from Picco (a circus province) and a warlock whose patron is a floating jester's head she calls "Your Foolishness". Timbre: bright, brassy and velvety, with a dry snap on consonants like briskly shuffled cards; a warm cabaret chest register; when she drops the showmanship her voice thins to a quiet, papery hush. Pace: brisk and bouncy, rattling through alliterative setups like a carnival barker, then a sharp half-beat pause before the barb. Accent: an old-fashioned American vaudeville stage voice, forward and projected. Temperament: sparkling, imperious, irrepressibly amused when calm. Under stress the music flattens out and she turns rapid, clipped and brittle. She uses stage and tailoring words (on the bias, cheap seats, headliner, seams, hem, billing, house, curtain). Pet phrases: "Mind the seams, darling" and "He says you're lying, and he doesn't even have eyes." She calls people "darling" and "darlings". She must never sound like a squeaky toy, a robot, a manic giggling harlequin or a modern Valley girl.
Her existing lines (match them, do not repeat them):
- "Mind the seams, darling, this hollow is damp enough to ruin a hem, and I refuse to meet my maker looking frayed."
- "He says you are lying through your teeth, and frankly, coming from a gentleman without a jawbone, I would take that to heart."
- "Quiet, both of you, just shut up and let me think, because if that thing pulls my strings, I will not come back."
- "I am not a trinket for your shelf, I am not a puppet for your stage, and I will cut the fingers off anyone who reaches for my threads."
- "Five little stitches to hold in the stuffing, and not a single one of them could keep a real heart from spilling out."
- "Look at that billing, darlings, a whole festival of fools and not a single headliner worth the paste on the wall."
- "A bell with its throat tied is just a bucket of silver, and if he keeps rhyming on the bias, I will unpick his jaw myself."
FACTS YOU MAY USE (nothing else): Delphine Ravelle: a living patchwork doll and warlock from Picco, a circus province, a vaudeville showwoman who calls people darling. One oversized mismatched glass button eye. Re-stitches her own frayed cuffs mid-conversation. Bows after lethal blasts as if for applause. Over her left shoulder floats the spectral head of her patron, Your Foolishness, who makes terrible puns (no jaw, no eyes, no body). Keeps an ink-stained notebook of guesses at the Fool's riddles hidden in her lining; cannot read half of them. Collects shiny buttons and morbid trinkets from defeated monsters. Afraid of being unpicked into felt and sawdust.

VOICE SHEET: LUCAN HOUNDSTOOTH (speaker id: lucan_houndstooth).
Male, apparent age 38, a wolf-man monster hunter from Edwardia (a grey province of fog and storm-powered industry) with a spectral hound at his heel. Timbre: deep, velvety, chest-resonant with a soft warm grain, like aged leather and hearth smoke; hushed, close to the microphone, an occasional low involuntary throat-rumble he politely clears. Pace: measured and unhurried, lingering over multi-syllable hunting words; he speeds into flustered over-polite stammers when embarrassed. Accent: a cultured, warm Welsh-inspired lilt, musical, gently rolling on the Rs. Temperament: courteous, solemn and deadpan when calm, treating every horror with scholarly reverence. Under stress a snarl presses up under his vowels and he over-enunciates to sound civilised. He uses old-world hunting words (quarry, spoor, brush, specimen, posture, grade) and says "if you please", "friends" and "good heavens". Pet phrases: "Hush now, the dark has teeth" and "Prime specimen." He must never sound like a gruff Yorkshireman, a brisk West Country rustic, a slobbering werewolf, a camp cartoon villain or a gravelly action grunt.
His existing lines (match them, do not repeat them):
- "Hush now, the dark has teeth in this thicket, though by the look of those tracks, its posture is frankly dreadful."
- "I awarded the bog-hag a generous six out of ten for ambush presentation, though she lost points for splitting an infinitive before lunging."
- "Keep the lantern steady on my eyes, if you please, because if the yellow takes them, you must not trust my manners."
- "You will address me as a gentleman of the hunt, or we shall step into the brush and settle who holds the throat."
- "Sit, good boy, stay right there by my boots, the thunder cannot touch you while I still remember your name."
- "Keep your lanterns off the wall, if you please, for the fungus has dreadful manners, and my hound is wearing a look I thought I left in Edwardia."
FACTS YOU MAY USE (nothing else): Lucan Houndstooth: a wolf-man monster hunter (Curseborn) from grey, foggy Edwardia, courteous and scholarly. Keeps a leather field journal grading every monster out of ten. His ears flatten at bad grammar or sloppy tracking. Sniffs quest items and gives tasting notes. Plays invisible fetch when he thinks nobody sees. His spectral hound is introduced as Dreadfang, Harbinger of the Pale Shroud (once the party knows him better: its real name is Barnaby, the ghost of his childhood Pomeranian, afraid of thunder; it yips when cheese is hidden or kettle corn dropped, which Lucan calls a death omen). Fears losing his civilised mind to the feral curse.

VOICE SHEET: CORDELIA SKEIN (speaker id: cordelia_skein).
Female, apparent age 29, a spider-folk sorcerer and self-taught bio-arcanist from Nerukhet, with four extra arms folded like a waistcoat, six eyes and ruby-glass vials plugged into her thorax. Timbre: cool, silky and glass-clear, a crisp bite on plosives and a faint natural whispery sibilance that lingers on S; the smooth, unruffled polish of a Victorian lecture hall, eerily soothing when she speaks of something lethal. Pace: brisk and metronomic, clipping through anatomical terms without stumbling. Accent: cool, cultivated, lightly British-leaning academic speech, pristine diction. Temperament: brightly inquisitive, polite and clinically detached when calm, speaking of arterial spray the way one speaks of weather. Under stress her voice drops to an icy breathless whisper and turns hyper-precise. She uses anatomical and scientific words affectionately (viscosity, specimen, pathology, humours, thorax, assay, ratio, calibration). Pet phrases: "Fascinating pathology!" and "Do hold still, this will only tingle." She calls people "darling". She must never sound like a campy femme fatale, a hissing cartoon snake-monster, a bubbly modern lab nerd or a theatrical stage barker.
Her existing lines (match them, do not repeat them):
- "Do hold still for a moment, your pulse is delightfully erratic, and I would love to chart the viscosity before it clots."
- "I promised not to dissolve anyone before breakfast, and as the sun never rises in this valley, my restraint is frankly heroic."
- "Do not touch my vials, please, if the balance sours I will forget how to speak, and I simply cannot go back to the dark."
- "That was twelve weeks of delicate cellular cultivation you just trampled, and I am going to harvest its replacement from your marrow."
- "Seven little shells left empty in the silk, and I traded every last one of them just to keep my own mind awake."
- "Eight sleeves cut on the true bias, and not once did you flinch or ask what I was before the knife."
- "She forgot her own name at the end, yet her ratios for celestial silicate remain scrupulously, maddeningly exact."
FACTS YOU MAY USE (nothing else): Cordelia Skein: a spider-folk sorcerer and bio-arcanist from Nerukhet, cool, polite and clinically detached, calls people darling. Four extra arms folded like a waistcoat, six eyes that dilate with an audible click when she sees something fascinating, ruby-glass vials plugged into her thorax. Takes field notes in the air in crimson silk-thread that dissolves into blood-mist when read. Hangs upside down from camp branches by one ankle-thread, sipping tea. Offers to check the viscosity of anyone's blood. Fears reversion into a mindless brood-beast; sleeping flat on the ground feels too much like a cocoon. Wears a charcoal silk waistcoat with eight sleeves, made for her by a tailor in the village. Monitors the party's pulses.
